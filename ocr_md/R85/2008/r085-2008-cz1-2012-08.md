Measuring system description:

The level sensor MTL-11 consists of shielded shaft which includes steel probe tube inside shaft, 2 floats and electronic at top of the shaft.

Due to movement of floats there is voltage induction which is evaluated by electronic at top of the shaft. The measured level is transferred to PC program by 4-wire connection (2-wire for data, 2-wire for supply) through the Zenner barrier.
