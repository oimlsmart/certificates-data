## Measuring system description:

The measuring system consists of magnetostrictive level probe(s) FMP-LL3-XXX and/or FMP-LL3- XXX-I with floats suitable for given application and one console unit EVO 200 or EVO 400 or EVO 600 or EVO 6000. When console unit EVO 600 or EVO 6000 is used then the system can contain one expansion box EVO-EXPC or EVO-EXPC2.
