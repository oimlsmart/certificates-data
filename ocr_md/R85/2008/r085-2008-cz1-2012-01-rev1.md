Measuring system description:

The level sensor HLS 6010 consists of shielded shaft that is in contact with measured medium and electronics at top of the shaft.

Normal cabinet dimensions of Supply: height 230 mm; width 300 mm; depth 85 mm.

The connection to the OptiLevel supply peripheral device is achieved by means of a Hectronic three-wire interface and permits cable lengths between the probe and the EX barrier of up to 250 m. The cable length for the serial connection between the supply and the evaluation device may not exceed 25 m.
