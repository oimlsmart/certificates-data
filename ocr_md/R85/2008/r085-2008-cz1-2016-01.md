## Measuring system description:

The level sensor VISY-Stick Advanced is done by a shaft in stainless steel (diameter 12 mm), by a head in stainless steel IP68, M12 connector and two floats in Stainless Steel.

The level sensor VISY-Stick Flex is done by a shaft and corrugated hose in stainless steel (diameter 12 mm), by a head in stainless steel IP68, M12 connector, two floats in Stainless Steel, weight and magnetic base at the lower end of the probe.
