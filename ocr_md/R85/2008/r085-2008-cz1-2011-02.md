This certificate attests the conformity of above identified type (represented by the sample or samples identified in the associated test report) with the requirements of the following Recommendation(s) of the International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML):

R 85, edition 2008

This certificate relates only to the metrological and technical characteristics of the type of instrument covered by the relevant OIML Recommendation(s) identified above.
