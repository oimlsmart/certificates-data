## Characteristics of the measuring instrument

In Table 1 the general characteristics of the measuring instrument are presented.

The construction of the measuring instrument is recorded in the Documentation folder number 2579596-1.

The automatic level gauge is intended to measure by servo principle automatically and display the level of the liquid contained in a tank. Herewith the following displacer types can be used:

- SF displacer with no additional sensors;

- TF displacer including temperature sensor;

- MF displacer including temperature sensor and density sensor.
