# Descriptive annex

The automatic multi-dimensional measuring instrument is designated MV-DLxxxx-0yy-y, x and y may be 0-9 or A-Z and represent models with different housing colour, baseline or scan rate.
Minimum scan rate is 600, higher is allowed.
The instrument is intended for scanning the dimensions of rectangular boxes moved on a transport belt below the camera.

The instrument can measure objects of different colours and sizes but does not measure irregular objects.

The instrument consists of a 3D laser camera placed on a portal or mounted in the ceiling. The pictures from the camera of the object, are transmitted to a PC, which analyses them and calculates the dimensions.

**Characteristics**

| | |
|---|---|
| Measuring mode: | dynamically |
| Maximum dimensions: | 1000 × 2000 × 1000 mm (W × L × H) |
| Scale interval (d): | 5 × 5 × 5 mm (W × L × H) |
| Minimum dimensions: | 50 × 50 × 50 mm (W × L × H) |
| Electromagnetic class: | E2 |
| Power supply: | 12-24 VDC |
| Temperature range: | +0°C to 45°C |
| Maximum speed of conveyor: | 3 m/s |

The MDMI does not measure irregular shape objects.

**Software**

The legal PC software versions is shown in the bottom of the user display.

The approved software version is V1.0.0 build 20260205

**Data Storage Device**

The PC software includes a data storage device working as an alibi memory in which all performed measurements are stored.

Alternate are measurement send to a central system.

**Interfaces**

- Ethernet interface
