**Characteristics of the multi-dimensional measuring instrument**

The VIPAC D xNVS uses two VOLUMEC HD 3.x sensor heads to record the dimension of objects. Measurement of objects on a conveyor band may be done dynamically or statically in a start-stop scenario.
