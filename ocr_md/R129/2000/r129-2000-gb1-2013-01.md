## Prime Components and Characteristics

Mounted to the metal chassis within the head are:

- Two cameras, one of which is fixed to a zoom lens;
- Three custom printed circuit boards
- 3D Sensor
- An OEM single-board computer, which includes other boards within its assembly

The associated software may be installed on any CE Marked PC.

If required any CE Marked LAN Hub may be used.
