This pattern of an automatic continuous totaliser (belt weigher), designed to weigh large quantities of loose material from bulk to bulk, is designated the "Milltronics MSI or MMI". It comprises:

- &nbsp;&nbsp; a weighing platform type MSI or MMI
- &nbsp;&nbsp; a speed sensor
- &nbsp;&nbsp; an integrator type BW 500

The MMI weighing platform comprises two or three MSI weighing platforms installed in succession.

Load cells:

The load cells are single-ended beam (bending) G4-TBSP models manufactured by Group Four Transducers, as described in OIML Certificate of Conformity R60/2000-GB-01.02.

The instrument is provided with the following devices:

- &nbsp;&nbsp; main totalising device
- &nbsp;&nbsp; semi-automatic zero-setting device
- &nbsp;&nbsp; indication of the flow rate (Q)
- &nbsp;&nbsp; indication of the belt speed
- &nbsp;&nbsp; indication of the totalised weight
- &nbsp;&nbsp; error messages and alarms
