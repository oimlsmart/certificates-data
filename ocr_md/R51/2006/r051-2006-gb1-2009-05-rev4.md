<u>Load cell</u>:

The weighing device comprises two strain gauge load cells located below the centre of the weigh conveyor. The load cells type may be as follows: Tedea Huntleigh 1042 C3, capacity according to technical data table.

Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:

- There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load cell.
- The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer's declaration of compatibility of modules and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.
- It is not a load cell with digital output
- The characteristics of the replacement load cell such as η<sub>lc</sub>, Y, Z are the same or better that the load cell tested dynamically (Tedea Huntleigh 1042 C3, capacity 5 kg)
- The design of the load cells and the material are the same
- No oil damper is used

<u>Devices</u>:

- Semi-automatic zero-setting (≤ 4% Max)
- Initial zero-setting (≤ 20% Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤ 4% Max)
- Automatic zero setting device active during automatic operation (at least every 35 min)
- Pre-set tare device (subtractive)
- Static calibration not accessible to the user
- Dynamic calibration (not accessible to the user), or dynamic setting functionality (recorded and available to the user)
- Belt speed setting (accessible to the user)
- Internal memory for storage of batch reports
- Device that acts upon significant faults
- Screen check at power-up

<u>Software</u>:

The general software is designated G6IJ, the legally relevant module is designated 9DE7. These software designations are shown in the start-up window as “software release” and “legal release”, respectively. The general software release may also be displayed in the upper left corner of the OPTIONS menu pages, which can be accessed from the main menu page by pressing the F5 function key and then F3.

<u>Sealings</u>:

Access to the legally relevant part of the software is password protected. Every time the metrological parameters or the calibration are changed an audit counter is incremented. This counter, designated “Access counter”, can be displayed by accessing the “WEIGHING OPTIONS” menu page, and should be written on a tamper-evident label located on or near the rating plate.

Access to the electronics (interface and display unit) is prevented by securing the enclosure with a seal bearing a securing mark. Components that may not be dismantled or adjusted by the user (load cell) will be secured by either a wire and seal or tamper evident label and securing mark.

The load cell serial numbers are indelibly written on the data plate.

<u>Alternatives</u>:

Having a modified software, where the general software is designated G6LN, the legally relevant module is designated 981A (displayed as per section Software).

Having the instrument designated the 08T3, 09T3, 10T3 and 14T3. The LCD display is replaced by a touch screen type T3.

The instrument may have a number of the following interfaces:

- RS 232
- USB (only for data collection on memory stick)
- Ethernet

The instruments may be fitted with a metal detector located on the in-feed conveyor. Any type may be used
