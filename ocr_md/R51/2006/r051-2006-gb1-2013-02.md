## **Characteristics of the instrument**:

This family of mains-powered, dual-range, automatic weighing instruments is designated the DACS-G-F015 Series; the instruments operate as automatic checkweighers (Category X).

The models are designated DACS-G-F015-xx/xx-xx-x, with x reflecting the various configurations

<u>Construction</u>:

The instruments are constructed in stainless steel. The framework is a fabricated floor standing stainless steel frame on adjustable feet. On the frame are mounted the conveyors (in-feed, weigh and out-feed). The conveyors' type and size are not restricted.

The control cabinet is located behind of the conveyors and houses the electrical hardware. An enclosure located below the weigh conveyor houses the ADC board and the Dip-Switches protecting the legally-relevant parameters. The Remote Control Unit is mounted in the upper part of the control cabinet. Photocells mounted on the frame are used for pack detection. A printer is located on the side of the control cabinet.

<u>Weighing unit</u>:

The weighing device comprises a force balance electromagnetic cell located below the centre of the weigh conveyor.

The el force balance electromagnetic cell is an FBi2-1500 type, manufactured by Ishida Japan.

Packs are weighed as they pass over the weigh head conveyor which runs continuously at the speed of the in-feed and out-feed conveyors.

<u>Electrical</u>:

The weighing controller comprises the A/D converter located on the weigh board, and processes the load cell signal. The controller send the processed weigh signal and photo cell unit signal the DRV board which controls the infeed/outfeed conveyors.

The instruments are fitted with a jog-dial RCU, comprising an LED display, function and numerical keys, jog-dial.

<u>Devices</u>:

- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% Max)
- Automatic zero-setting devices (≤ 4% Max)
    - ▪ Every 18 min 50 s (Range 1) or 37 min 40 s (Range2)
    - ▪ When no pack has been detected
- Initial zero-setting (≤ 20% Max)
- Determination of stability of equilibrium
- Preset tare device
- Static calibration (not available to the user)
- Dynamic calibration (available to the user, recorded)
