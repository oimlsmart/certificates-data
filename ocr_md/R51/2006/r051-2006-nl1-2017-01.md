## OIML R 51 - Edition 2006 (E) for accuracy class Y(a)

This Certificate relates only to the metrological and technical characteristics of the type of measuring instrument covered by the relevant OIML International Recommendation above-identified.

This Certificate does not bestow any form of legal international approval.
