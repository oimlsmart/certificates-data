Characteristics: This pattern of a vehicle-mounted, battery-operated automatic catchweighing instrument comprises an electronic indicator, hydraulic pressure transducers and sensors. It automatically determines the load in the vehicle's bucket during the lifting process. The hydraulic pressure in the loader's arms is measured by a pressure transducer, the output being used by the indicator to display the corresponding weight. The indicator may be connected to a printer and/or a data storage device.
