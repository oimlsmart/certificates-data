The load cells (LC) of the series PR6224/.. are compact compression load cells for self-centering pendulum applications. The strain gauge application is hermetically sealed; the deep-drawn and micro plasma welded housing is made of stainless steel and filled with inert gas. The analog signal of the strain gauge bridge is amplified, scaled and filtered by the integrated modul. The load cell is equipped with an interface RS485.
