The load cells of the series PR 6204 are compact compression load cells for self-centering pendu-lum applications. The strain gauge application is hermetically sealed; the deep-drawn and micro plasma welded housing is made of stainless steel and filled with inert gas. The analog signal of the strain gauge bridge is amplified, scaled and filtered by the integrated module. The load cell is equipped with an interface RS485.

The load can only be used in combination with a data processing device of manufacturer Sartorius.

The metrological characteristics for application in approved weighing instruments are listed in table 1.
