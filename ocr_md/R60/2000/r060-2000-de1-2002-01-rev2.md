Identification of the pattern (continued)

Load cells of the type C16 are compression load cells for self centering pendulum applications. Using the fitting elements of the manufacturer, the load cell is fixed against rotation. The one column load cell body and the housing are made of stainless steel. The strain-gauge application is hermetically sealed.
