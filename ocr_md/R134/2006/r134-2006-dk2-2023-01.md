## Sealing

Access to the configuration and calibration facilities of the LCA indicator is achieved by removing an internal calibration jumper, which is located on the Scale Input Card. Access is prohibited when the jumper is applied.

Sealing of the access to the jumper is accomplished by either sealing the enclosure with either wire and seal through holes in the fixing screws of the enclosure or by means of tamper evident stickers.

The cable between the load receptor and the indicator shall be sealed with wire and seal or with tamper evident sticker(s)
