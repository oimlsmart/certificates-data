## Load cells

The instrument uses one load cell type Flintec PC1 C3 $ \left( E_{\mathrm{m a x}}=30 \mathrm{~kg} \right) $ located in the centre of the weighing conveyor.

Any analogue load cells may be used for this instrument provided the following conditions are met:

1) There is an OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60:2000 or R60:2017) issued for the load cell

2) The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer's declaration of compatibility of modules (OIML R76:2006 Annex F), and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to OIML R76:2006 has been conducted on this load cell.

3) The compatibility of load cells and indicator is established by the manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules form, according to OIML R76:2006 Annex F.

4) The load cell transmission must be a standard, non-critical design.

## Sealing Measures

The indicator and the junction box for load cells shall be sealed against access to their internal by sealing using wire and seal or tamper-evident stickers.

Detachable load cell connectors shall be sealed using tamper-evident stickers.
