# Page 3 of 7 — OIML Certificate R49/2024-A-SK1-26.01

This page covers the **Designation** and **Description** sections of the certificate for the MA408 ultrasonic water meter. Here's a summary of its contents:

## 1. Designation
- The **MA408** ultrasonic water meter measures, memorizes, and displays the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers at metering conditions; intended for clean water in residential use.
- It is a residential compact ultrasonic meter with an electronic indication device, based on the **ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle** — flow is measured by the difference in time-of-flight of ultrasonic pulses downstream vs. upstream.
- Can be installed in **horizontal and vertical positions**; not designed to measure reverse flow.

## 2. Description

**Essential parts:**
- *Flow sensor:* brass cylindrical body with inlet/outlet, inner plastic measuring supports (pipe support-down/up), two reflection sheets in the pipe centre, and two ultrasonic transducers (upstream and downstream).
- *Calculator and indication device:* plastic housing mounted directly on the flow sensor, one PCB (main + measurement board combined), electronic scrolling LCD with 9 digits (range 999999,999 m³), and one non-replaceable lithium battery (lifetime 18 years).

**Non-essential parts:** strainer, optical communication port, one communication battery.

## 2.1 Metrological Functions
- Measuring, memorizing, and displaying the volume of water passing through the meter.

## 2.2 Operation and Presentation of Legal Data
- Displays total measured volume (m³) and flow rate (m³/h).
- Scroll display also shows: display test ("eights" test), display test ("blanks" test), forward consumption volume (m³), and last value for charge (m³) with date (MM-DD-YY).

The page bears the **Slovak Legal Metrology (SLM)** official stamp.
