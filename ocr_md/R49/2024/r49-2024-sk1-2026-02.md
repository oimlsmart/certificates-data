## 1. Designation

The water meter type VK-12 is designed to measure, memorize and display the volume of water passing through the measuring chamber at metering conditions. The water meters are intended for the measurement of clean water volume in residential use.

The mechanical volumetric (rotary piston) water meter VK-12 is water meter with dry type mechanical indication device.
The water meter VK-12 can be installed to operate in horizontal and vertical positions and is not designed to measure the reverse flow.

## 2. Description

Essential parts of the water meter type VK-12:

- measuring mechanism – consisting of measuring chamber assembled with a rotary piston and top plate with transmission shaft for connection of measuring part with register;
- dry type mechanical register and indication device with:
  - 8 numbered drums (last significant drum moves continuously), 5 drums black colour for cubic meters, 3 drums red colour for sub-multiples of a cubic meter;
  - 1 continuously moving rotating pointer with marked 0,00005 sub-multiples of a cubic meter;
- magnetic coupling for the connection of the register part with the measuring mechanism (rotary piston);
- the brass body with inlet and outlet connections.

Non-essential parts of water meter:

- sieve (in the inlet tube of water meter);
- non-return valve (in the outlet tube of water meter);
- coupling (in the inlet and outlet connection of water meter);
- external optional device - a remote-controlled valve without a measuring function and without data transmission, used to open/close the water flow.

### 2.1 Metrological functions

- measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.

## 3. Integrated equipment and functions

- RF, M-Bus, Modbus, GPRS or Smart card and Pulse output (optionally).

## 5. Marking and inscriptions

The following data shall be marked on the water meter:

- a) name or trademark of the manufacturer;
- b) type name of the water meter;
- c) unit of measurement m<sup>3</sup>;
- d) year of manufacture, the last two digits of the year of manufacture, or the month and year of manufacture;
- e) serial number (as near as possible to the indicating device);
- f) direction of flow, by means of an arrow (shown on both sides of the body or on one side only provided the direction of flow arrow is easily visible under all circumstances);
- g) flowrate Q<sub>3</sub> and ratio Q<sub>3</sub>/Q<sub>1</sub> indicated as (R) followed by the ratio value;
- h) H/V – water meter can operate in the horizontal and vertical position;
- i) maximum admissible pressure (MAP);
- j) temperature class (T);
- k) environmental classification (can be given on a document supplied separately);
- l) electromagnetic environmental class (can be given on a document supplied separately);
- m) type approval sign according to national regulations.

## 6. Security measures

The water meter type VK-12 shall be protected against unauthorised manipulation by:

- one twist or lead or plastic seal securing the connection of the water meter head with the body; or
- a security circle (it is seal) between plastic upper body and lower body in order to prevent the water meter against unauthorized interference.
