**Characteristics of the measuring instrument**  
In Table 1 the general characteristics of the measuring instrument are presented.  
The cylindrical measuring tube of the measurement sensor can have a reduced bore (type FLC-2300) or a full bore (type FLC-2200EL).  
Table 2 and 3 gives an overview of the general characteristics of the family of instruments.  
The construction of the measuring instrument is recorded in the Documentation folder no. T11690-0.
