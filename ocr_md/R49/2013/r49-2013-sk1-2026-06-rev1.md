**1. Designation**

The ultrasonic water meters **AFlow LXC** are designed to measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers at metering conditions. The water meter is intended for the measurement of volume of clean water in residential use.

The water meters AFlow LXC are compact ultrasonic water meters with electronic indication device. The measurement is based on ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle.<br>
The flow is measured by the difference in time-of-flight of ultrasonic pulses with flow (downstream) and opposite to flow (upstream).

The water meters AFlow LXC can be installed to operate in all positions. The water meters are designed to measure the reverse flow.

**2. Description**

Essential parts of the water meters AFlow LXC:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;the brass and composite cylindrical body with inlet and outlet;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;the inner plastic elements-measuring support (pipe support-down and pipe support-up) placed in the cylindrical brass body;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;two reflectors (Inlet, Outlet) installed in the centre of the water meter body (pipe);<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;two ultrasonic transducers at the upstream and downstream of the measurement channel (pipe section) to transmit and receive ultrasonic signals.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;the plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;two PCBA:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o &nbsp;&nbsp;measuring PCBA located on the water meter body above the transducers;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o &nbsp;&nbsp;main PCBA located under LCD display, for function of LCD display, button, optical port, wireless communication, memory, battery;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;the electronic scrolling LCD display with 10 digits and indication range of 999999,999 m³. The sub-multiples of a cubic meter are indicated on the LCD display with a frame above the six digits after the decimal point;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;one non-replaceable lithium battery for measurement, size D, Umin=2,9 V, Umax=3,7 V, lifetime 18 years;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;button “Touch“ for switching display functions.

Non-essential parts of the water meters:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;optical (Infrared) interface for local management and maintenance;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;filter;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;pressure sensor (optionally);<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;temperature sensor (optionally);<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;one non-replaceable backup lithium battery for measurement, size 1/2AA, Umin = 2,9 V, Umax = 3,7 V, lifetime18 years;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- &nbsp;&nbsp;communication interfaces: optical, pulse, RS485, Wired M-Bus, Wireless M-Bus, LoRaWAN, Sigfox, GPRS, NB-IoT, dual module.
