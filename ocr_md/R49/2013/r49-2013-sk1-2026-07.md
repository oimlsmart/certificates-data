**1. Designation**

The ultrasonic water meter types **U550 / Q400V** (models U550-15K, U550-20K, U550-25K, U550-32K or Q400V) are designed to measure, memorize and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers under metering conditions. The water meters are intended for the measurement of the volume of clean water in residential, commercial and light industrial use.

The water meter types U550 / Q400V are compact ultrasonic water meters with an electronic indication device. The measurement is based on the ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle. The water meter can be installed in horizontal and vertical positions and is not designed to measure reverse flow.

**2. Description**

Essential parts of the water meter types U550 / Q400V:

- Water meter body:
  - the stainless-steel cylindrical body firmly connected together with the plastic housing for the calculator.
- Flow sensor:
  - two reflection sheets and two ultrasonic transducers at the upstream and downstream of the measurement channel (pipe section) to transmit and receive ultrasonic signals.
- Calculator and indication device:
  - the plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;
  - the PCB board;
  - the electronic scrolling LCD display with 9 digits and indication range of 99999,9999 m³;
  - the decimal values behind the decimal point are framed around during the displaying of the total consumption (m³);
  - one replaceable lithium battery (U<sub>min</sub> = 3,2 V; U<sub>max</sub> = 3,7 V; 15 years battery lifetime) for measuring functions.

Non-essential parts of the water meter types U550 / Q400V:

- filter;
- strainer;
- valve + actuator for valve;
- one replaceable lithium battery for wireless communication functions;
- communication module with optional wireless communication interfaces: LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, GPS, wM-bus.

**2.1 Metrological functions**

– measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.

**2.2 Operation and presentation of legal data**

The legal data available on the electronic indication device (LCD display) in user mode:
a) the total measured volume of water (0,0001 m³);
b) flow rate (0,001 m³/h);
c) checksum;
d) software version.

The legal data available on the electronic indication device (LCD display) in test mode:
e) the total measured volume (0,000001 m³);
f) flow rate (0,001 m³/h);
g) checksum;
h) software version.

a) name or trademark of the manufacturer;
b) type name of the water meter;
c) unit of measurement m³;
d) year of manufacture, the last two digits of the year of manufacture, or the month and year of manufacture;
e) serial number (as near as possible to the indicating device);
f) flowrate Q<sub>3</sub> and ratio Q<sub>3</sub>/Q<sub>1</sub> indicated as (R) followed by the ratio value;

i) temperature class (T);
j) pressure loss class (Δp);
k) the latest date by which the battery shall be replaced;
