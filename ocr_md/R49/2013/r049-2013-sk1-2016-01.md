## 1. Designation

The mechanical multi-jet wet dial water meters type *ML-B* and *ML-BP* are designed to measure, memorise and display the volume at metering conditions of water passing through the measurement transducer. They are intended for the measurement of volumes of clean cold water in household or a residential use. The mechanical water meters type *ML-B* and *ML-BP* are multi jet rotary vane wheel water meters with the mechanical indication device.
The water meter type *ML-B* has a brass body. The water meters type *ML-BP* has a plastic body. The water meters types *ML-B* and *ML-BP* shall be installed to operate in horizontal position with the indication device positioned at the top.

## 2. Description

Essential parts of the water meter:

- measuring mechanism - consisting of a measuring chamber and the rotary vane wheel (impeller) with an axle perpendicular to the flow direction;
- wet mechanical register with gearing mechanism combined with 5 numbered drums (the drum register is immersed in the special type liquid) and 4 continuously moving rotating pointers. The transmission from the impeller to the gears of the reading mechanism is direct.
- housing of the water meter with inlet and outlet connections;
- adjustment device – an adjustment screw regulates the internal by-pass flow of the meter.

Non-essential parts of water meter:

- sieve in the inlet of the meter;
- non - return valve (optional).

### 2.1 Metrological functions

- measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of the water passing through the water meter

### 2.2 Software

- not applicable

### 2.3 Integrated equipment and functions

- pulse output (optional).

## 5. Marking and inscriptions

The following data shall be marked on the water meter:

- a) manufacturer's name or mark;
- b) type of water meter;
- c) measuring unit m³;
- d) year of production and serial number;
- e) flowrateQ<sub>3</sub> and ratio Q<sub>3</sub>/Q<sub>1</sub> indicated as (R) followed by the ratio;
- f) maximum working pressure, indicated as MAP 16;
- g) maximum water temperature, indicated as T50;
- h) indication that the meter must be installed horizontally (H);
- i) OIML Certificate of conformity number.

The flow direction shall be marked on a water meter's body in form of an arrow.
Markings on water meter must comply with the requirements OIML R 49.

Manufacturer can use following trademarks on its water meters:

The water meter shall be protected against unauthorised manipulation by one seal securing the water meter head with the screw cap of adjustment device.
