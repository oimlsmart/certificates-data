# OIML Certificate R49/2013-A-SK1-26.08 — Page 3 Summary

## 1. Designation
- **Type:** LXCZ ultrasonic water meter (family DN15, DN20, DN25)
- **Purpose:** Measures, memorizes, and displays the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers; intended for clean water volume measurement in residential use
- **Technology:** Residential compact ultrasonic water meter with electronic indication device, using ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle; flow measured by double-pulse system with positive/negative pulses depending on flow direction
- **Installation:** Horizontal position with display top, or vertical position with flow up; **not designed to measure reverse flow**

## 2. Description

**Essential parts:**
- **Water meter body:** Plastic cylindrical body connected with plastic calculator housing; inner plastic elements (pipe support-down and pipe support-up)
- **Flow sensor:** Two reflection sheets in centre of cylindrical plastic body; two ultrasonic transducers at upstream/downstream of measurement channel
- **Calculator and indication device:**
  - Plastic housing with indication device mounted directly on flow sensor
  - Two PCB boards (metering PCB = core, unchanged; main PCB = service applications and external communication)
  - Electronic scrolling LCD with 9 digits, indication range 999999,999 m³
  - Replaceable lithium battery (Umax: 3.6 V; 19 Ah; 10-year lifetime)

**Non-essential parts:** filter; control valve; strainer; wireless interfaces (IR, Bluetooth, WM-Bus, LoRa, LoRaWAN, NB-IoT Standard, GPRS, Cat.1, Sigfox)

## 2.1 Metrological Functions
- Measuring, memorizing, and displaying water volume passing through the meter

## 2.2 Operation and Presentation of Legal Data
Automatic scroll display shows:
- a) total measured volume (m³)
- b) flow rate (m³/h)
- c) water temperature (°C)
- d) legal software version
- e) legal software checksum
