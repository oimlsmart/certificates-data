**1.&nbsp;&nbsp;Designation**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The mechanical multi-jet water meters type LXSG-15 is designed to measure, memorise and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducer at metering conditions. The water meter is intended for the measurement of volumes of clean water in residential use.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The mechanical water meter type LXSG-15 is multi-jet rotary vane wheel water meters with dry mechanical indication device and consist of a brass body.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter type LXSG-15 shall be installed to operate in the horizontal position only with the indication device positioned at the top and is not designed to measure the reverse flow.

**2.&nbsp;&nbsp;Description**

Essential parts of the water meter type LXSG-15:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;measuring mechanism – consisting of the measuring chamber and the rotary vane wheel (impeller) with an axle perpendicular to the flow direction;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;dry type mechanical register and indication device with 5 numbered black colour drums for cubic meters and 4 continuously moving rotating red colour pointers for sub-multiples of a cubic meters;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;housing of water meter with inlet and outlet connections;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;magnetic coupling for the connection of mechanical register with the measuring mechanism;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;adjustment device – an adjustment screw regulates the internal by-pass flow of the meter;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;filter fitted at the inlet of a meter.

**3.&nbsp;&nbsp;Metrological functions**

Metrological functions of water meter type LXSG-15:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter

**4.&nbsp;&nbsp;Integrated equipment and functions**

Integrated equipment and functions of water meter type LXSG-15:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;not applicable.
