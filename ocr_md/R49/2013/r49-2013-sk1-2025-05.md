**1. Designation**

The water meters type **SCL-61HF-100** is designed to measure, memorise and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducer at metering conditions. The water meter is intended for the measurement of volume of clean water in residential use.

The water meter type SCL-61HF-100 is compact ultrasonic water meter with electronic indication device. The measurement is based on ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle.
The flow is measured by the difference in time-of-flight of ultrasonic pulses with flow (downstream) and opposite to flow (upstream).

The water meter type SCL-61HF-100 can be installed to operate in the horizontal position with indication device on the top or in the vertical position.
The water meter type SCL-61HF-100 is not designed for measurement of reverse flow.

**2. Description**

**2.1 Parts of the water meter type SCL-61HF-100:**

Essential parts of the water meter:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;cylindrical brass body with inlet and outlet threaded connections;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the inner plastic element (casing pipe) placed in the cylindrical brass and in the plastic body;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;two reflection sheeds installed in the center of the pipe at an angle of 45 degrees with the axis of the pipe section;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;two ultrasonic transducers at the upstream and downstream of the measurement channel (pipe section) to transmit and receive ultrasonic signals.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;main PCB board with LCD display and optical sensor;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;non-replaceable lithium battery. The end of battery life indicator is activated when the battery voltage is below 3,6 V, lifetime 13 years;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;electronic LCD display (scrolling with using of optical sensor) with 10 digits and indication range of 19999,99999 m³;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The sub-multiples of a cubic meter are indicated on the display by the comma under numbers and above the numbers. When the maximum indication range of the volume totalization is reached, the indication range will continue measuring starting from zero cubic meter.

Non-essential parts of the water meter type SCL-61HF-100:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;thermistor;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;valve (optionally).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.
