**1. Designation**

The water meters types **LXC-15, LXC-20 and LXC-25** are designed to measure, memorise and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducer at metering conditions. The water meters are intended for the measurement of volume of clean water in residential use.

The water meter types LXC-15, LXC-20 and LXC-25 are compact ultrasonic water meter with electronic indication device. The measurement is based on ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle.<br>
The flow is measured by the difference in time-of-flight of ultrasonic pulses with flow (downstream) and opposite to flow (upstream).

The water meter types LXC-15, LXC-20 and LXC-25 can be installed to operate in the horizontal position with indication device on the top or in the vertical position. The water meters are not designed to measure the reverse flow.

**2. Description**

Essential parts of the water meter type LXC-15, LXC-20 and LXC-25:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:

- the brass cylindrical body with inlet and outlet firmly connected with the plastic housing for the calculator;
- the inner plastic elements placed in the cylindrical brass body with two reflection sheeds installed in the centre of the pipe;
- two ultrasonic transducers at the upstream and downstream of the measurement channel (pipe section) to transmit and receive ultrasonic signals;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:

- the plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;
- magnetic sensing area on the box - magnetic buttons or light photo sensitive buttons to swich the display menu;
- the PCB board;
- the electronic scrolling LCD display with:
  - 8 digits and indication range of 99999.999 m³. The sub-multiples of a cubic meter are indicated on the LCD display by three digits marked by line above numbers (LXC-15 and LXC-20)
  - 9 digits and indication range of 999999.999 m³. The sub-multiples of a cubic meter are indicated on the LCD display by three digits marked by frame around numbers (LXC-25);
- one non-replaceable lithium battery for metering. The battery voltage is below 3,6 V, lifetime 10 years
- magnet for to switch between different menus on the display (supplied together with water meters).

Non-essential parts of the water meters:

- communication types: NB-IOT/Lora/RS485 wired/M-bus (optionally);
- wired or wireless data communication port;
- antenna (optionally);
- M-bus for upstream;
- Infrared for downstream.

–&nbsp;&nbsp;measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.

**2.2 Operation and presentation of legal data**

With a magnet, it is possible to switch the display between different menus (on the magnetic sensing surface on the box).<br>
There are four menu groups for operation and display:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;main menu<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;s/n menu<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;time menu<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;software version menu

The following are visible on the displays:

mandatory:

- accumulated volume (m³);
- flow rate (m³/h);
- display test (an “eights” test);
- display test (a “blanks” test);
- software version number and checksum;
- “VER”verification mode (L)

informative:

- serial number;
- water temperature (°C);
- date (year-month day);
- time (hour-minute-second);
- error number
