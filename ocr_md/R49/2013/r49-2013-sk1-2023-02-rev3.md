**1.  Designation**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ultrasonic water meter type QALCOSONIC W1 is designed to measure, memorise and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducer at metering conditions. The water meter is intended for the measurement of volume of clean water in residential use and is intended for non-resettable measurements between two constant partners.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter type QALCOSONIC W1 is compact ultrasonic water meter with electronic indication device. The measurement is based on ultrasonic measurement method, bidirectional transit-time principle. Ultrasonic signal moves along the measuring section many times and the flow downstream between the ultrasonic sensors have to perform transmitter and receiver functions. From the results of time difference the flow rate is calculated and indicated in display.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter type QALCOSONIC W1 can be installed to operate in horizontal and vertical positions and is not designed to measure the reverse flow.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter QALCOSONIC W1 is produced in A design (for all DNs) and design B (for DN15 and DN20 only).<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The difference between A design and B design lies solely in the external appearance. The measurement section is identical.

**2.  Description**

**2.1  Parts of the water meter type QALCOSONIC W1**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Essential parts of the water meter:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the plastic cylindrical brass body with inlet and outlet firmly connected with the plastic housing for the calculator;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the inner plastic element with two mirrors (four mirrors for DN40) (sound path) placed in the cylindrical plastic body;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;two ultrasonic transducers (four for DN40) placed on the upside of the cylindrical plastic body.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the PCB boards - tree PCB and one PCB with calculator;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;the electronic LCD display with 2-lines:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;upper line with 9 digits and indication range of 999999.999 m³. The sub-multiples of a cubic meter are indicated on the LCD display by tree smaller digits after decimal point;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;bottom line with 5 digits to display the current flow rate in m³/h.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;non-replaceable lithium batteries, life 16 years. The end of battery life is visible on plate of water meter.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;–&nbsp;&nbsp;Batteries:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;for DN15, DN20, DN25, DN32 two internal batteries „AA:size“;<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;for DN15 and DN15 B design, DN20 and DN20 B design, DN25, DN32: two internal batteries „AA“ size + one „A23“;<br>
