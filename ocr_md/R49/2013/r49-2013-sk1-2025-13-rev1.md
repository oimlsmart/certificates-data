1. **Designation**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ultrasonic water meter type **LXCZ** (family DN15, DN20, DN25) is designed to measure, memorize and display the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers at metering conditions. The water meters are intended for the measurement of clean water volume in residential use.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter LXCZ is residential compact ultrasonic water meter with electronic indication device. The measurement is based on ultrasonic bidirectional transit-time principle. The flow is measured by the double-pulse system with positive and negative pulses depending on the flow direction.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter LXCZ can be installed to operate in horizontal position and vertical position. The water meter is not designed to measure the reverse flow.

2. **Description**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Essential parts of the water meters LXCZ:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Water meter body:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- the brass cylindrical body firmly connected together with the plastic housing for the calculator;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- the inner plastic elements (pipe support-down and pipe support-up) placed in the brass body.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- two reflection sheets installed in the centre of the cylindrical brass body (pipe section);
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- two ultrasonic transducers at the upstream and downstream of the measurement channel (pipe section) to transmit and receive ultrasonic signals.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- the plastic housing of the calculator with indication device directly mounted on the flow sensor;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- the PCB board;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- the electronic scrolling LCD display with 8 digits and indication range of 99999,999 m3;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- digits behind the decimal point are marked with white line above them which is printed on plastic housing;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- one replaceable lithium battery (Umax: 3,6 V; nominal capacity: 19 Ah, 10 years battery lifetime).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Non-essential parts of the water meter LXCZ:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- filter;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- internal valve;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- motor for valve;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- strainer;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- remote controller for communication;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- pressure sensor (optionally);
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- two non-replaceable batteries for water meters with pressure sensor;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- wireless communication interfaces: IR – infrared communication, Bluetooth, WM-Bus, LoRa, LoRaWAN, NB-IoT Standard, GPRS, Cat.1 and Sigfox;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- pre-payment system – utility provider can activate or deactivate pre-payment and additional functions like alarms and valve (open/close) conditions before water meter installation.

2.1 **Metrological functions**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- measuring, memorizing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.
