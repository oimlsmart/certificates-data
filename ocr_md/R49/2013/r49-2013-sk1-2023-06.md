**1. Designation**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The electromagnetic water meter **YYD-S** is designed to measuring, storing and displaying the volume of water passing through the measurement transducers at metering conditions. The water meter is intended for the measurement of volume of clean water in residential use.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meters YYD-S is residential compact electromagnetic water meter with electronic indication device.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The measurement is based on electromagnetic principle.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;According to Faraday’s law of electromagnetic induction a conductive liquid flow through an insulated pipe, generating a voltage signal at electrodes on both sides of the pipe under the action of internal magnetic field and is proportional to the fluid flow rate. The signal is amplified and filtered for accumulation - recording and output.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter YYD-S can be installed to operate in horizontal position. When the instrument is installed on the pipeline, the instrument must be equipped with a ground electrode or a ground ring (Fig. 5).  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The water meter is designed to measure the reverse flow.

**2. Description**

**2.1 Parts of the water meter YYD-S**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Essential parts:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Flow sensor:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– the electromagnetic transducer - the stainless-steel body with inlet and outlet;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– the two magnets for magnetic field and two measurement electrodes;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– the grounding wire on the flanges connected with the grounding device.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Calculator and indication device:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– the stainless-steel converter base connected with the electromagnetic transducer;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– the converter includes:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o the PCB circuit board for transfer between sensors and the circuit boards;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o the PCB circuit board for calculation and output;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o the electronic LCD display with two lines (first line with 9 digits and indication range 99999,9999 m³ for volume, second line with 5 digits m³/h for flow). The sub-multiples of a cubic meters are indicated on the LCD display after the decimal point and are indicated by a frame;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o magnet with menu operation button;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o the battery box - 4 pcs replaceable lithium batteries for flow rate (3,6 V and 76 Ah) and 2 pcs replaceable lithium batteries for data collector (3,6 V and 38 Ah), life of batteries 6 years.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Non-essential parts:  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– communication type RS485;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– antenna.

**2.2 Metrological functions**

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;– measuring, storing and displaying the volume of water passing through the water meter.
