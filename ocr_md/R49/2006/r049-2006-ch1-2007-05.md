**1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Description of the type**

The meter is a concentric multi-jet impeller meter with threading of 2”. The measuring sensor and the register are tightly interconnected to prevent any unauthorized opening and manipulation of the meter.
