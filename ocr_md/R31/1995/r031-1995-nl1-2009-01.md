This Certificate attests the conformity of the above identified type (represented by the samples identified in the associated Test Report) with the requirements of the following Recommendation of the International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML):

## R31

Edition 1995 (E)

This Certificate relates only to the metrological and technical characteristics of the type of instrument covered by the relevant OIML Recommendation identified above.

This Certificate does not bestow any form of legal international approval.

The conformity was established by tests described in the associated Test Report: $ N^{\circ} $ R31/1995-NL1-07.03 that includes 15 pages.

The design of the above mentioned gas meters is identical to the type of the gas meters as described in the OIML Certificate of Conformity N $ ^{\circ} $ R31/1995-NL1-07.03, manufactured by the company i-meter. The manufacturer produces the diaphragm gas meters based on a license agreement with the company i-meter, for whom the above mentioned OIML Certificate of Conformity was issued previously.
