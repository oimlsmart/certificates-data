Characteristics: This indicating device, designated the 820i or 920i indicator, has the following devices:

- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking device (≤0.5d/s within 4% Max)
- Subtractive tare device
- Gross and Net Indicator
- Semi automatic tare device
- Pre set tare device
- Display test device
- Time and date function

Comprising of: The indicator housing is fabricated from stainless steel plate. The front panel has a backlit LCD display and a twenty-seven key keyboard, five of the twenty seven keys are programmable software keys.
