Identification of the pattern (continued)

The weighing instrument consists of a weighing platform with one load cell of electromagnetic force compensa-tion (class (Ⅰ)) or one strain-gauge load cell (class (Ⅱ) and (Ⅲ)) and of an incorporated indicating device for displaying the weighing results, and of a keypad to operate the instrument.

The weighing ranges with Max, Min, e, d and number of verification scale intervals may be chosen within the limits of No. 3.2 of R 76-1 and of the table 1.
