Identification of the pattern (continued)
The weighing instrument consists of
- a weighing platform with strain-gauge load cell and / or a weighing module, comprising the load receptor, the strain gauge load cell or the electromagnetic force compensation load cell
- the electronics with AD-converter for signal processing the microprocessor-system with software module „NT-Scale“ for further signal processing and control of the balance operation, LC-display, indicating weight, tare values and additional information

The weighing ranges with Max, Min, e, d and number of verification scale intervals may be chosen within the limits of No. 3.2 of R 76-1 and of Table 1.
