Characteristics: This instrument is a Class III, mains or battery-operated, self-indicating, single-interval, non-automatic weighing instrument, designated the AWB120

Main features:

- Pole-mounted LCD display with function keys and LED indicators (low battery, checkweighing) (Figure 2)
- Indicator plastic enclosure
- Aluminium die-casting load receptor (420x520 mm) with stainless steel pan
- Level indicator (at the base of the pole) and adjustable feet

Devices:

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Net indicator
- Determination of stable equilibrium
- Stable equilibrium indicator
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing device
- Checking of display
- Weight accumulation
- Printing
- Checkweighing
- Coarse filter mode to allow weighing of unstable samples
