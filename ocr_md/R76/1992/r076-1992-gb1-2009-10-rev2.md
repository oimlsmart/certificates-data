Characteristics: &nbsp;&nbsp;The AP PLUS Series, AP PLUS Model (and variants) and AD PLUS Series, AD PLUS Model, are Class III, mains-operated, self-indicating, price-computing, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments.

Main features:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Steel construction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Operator's keypad:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- AP PLUS-EX Model: 52 keys, alphanumerical
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- AP PLUS-MX Model: 27 keys, alphanumerical
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- AP PLUS-M Model: 27 keys, alphanumerical
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- AP PLUS-EC Model: 45 keys, alphanumerical
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- AD PLUS Model: 4 keys
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Stainless steel load receptor
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- VFD display (AD PLUS Model)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Pole-mounted double-sided VFD display (AP PLUS Model)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Level indicator

Load cell: &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The load cell is CAS load cell, model BC.

Technical data: &nbsp;The instrument operates on a 110 or 240 Vac mains power supply.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Temperature-range: -10 to +40 °C
