**Characteristics:**

The A & D Mercury Model FS-30Ki is a Non Automatic Weighing Instrument of 30 kg maximum capacity. The instrument has the additional functionality of displaying 'LO/OK/HI' limits on the indicator for check weighing but is not included in this approval. The instrument has a stainless steel weigh platform and a pole mounted backlit LCD display.

The instrument may also work on a 6V sealed lead acid battery. The instrument also has serial interface connectivity for providing connections to peripheral devices. The peripheral devices are not covered under this approval.

The instrument was powered by mains power supply (220 – 240 V AC).

The instrument has capabilities for tare and preset-tare (including pre-set tare values associated with product look-up tables).

**Levelling**

The instrument has a level bubble and four adjustable feet for levelling.
