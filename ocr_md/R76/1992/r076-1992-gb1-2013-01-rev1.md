## Characteristics of the instruments:

This family of instruments is designated the PR Series, and comprises the PR PLUS-B and PR PLUS-P models.

The instruments are Class III, mains or battery-operated, self-indicating, price-computing, dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. The instruments are designed to be used for direct sales to the public.

<u>Construction</u>:

- Plastic construction
- Operator's keypad
- Stainless steel load receptor
- Base-mounted operator and customer LED displays (PR PLUS-B)
- Pole-mounted operator and customer LED displays (PR PLUS-P)
- Level indicator

<u>Devices</u>:

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Net indicator
- Stable weight indicator
- Low battery indicator
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing device
- Gravity compensation
- Price-computing
- PLU
- Calibration / set-up mode via sealed internal switch

<u>Load cell</u>:

The load cell is a DONGGUAN SOUTH CHINA SEA ELECTRONIC CO.,LTD load cell, model CZL-601 Series, capacities as per following table.
