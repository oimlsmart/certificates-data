**Characteristics of the instrument:**

This family of instruments is designated the CL5200J Series, and comprises the CL5200J-B (Figure 1) and CL5200J-P (Figure 2) models. The instruments are Class III, mains-operated, self-indicating, price-computing, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. The instruments are designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Construction</u>:

- Plastic construction
- Operator’s keypad
- Stainless steel load receptor
- Front and rear LCD displays (CL5200J-B)
- Pole-mounted double-sided LCD displays (CL5200J-P)
- Level indicator
- Integrated printer

<u>Devices</u>:

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Net indicator
- Stable weight indicator
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Preset tare
- Gravity compensation
- Price-computing
- Totalisation (including non-weighed items)
- PLU
- Fixed weight labelling
- Multi-vendor operation
- Calibration / set-up mode via sealed internal switch
- Self-service operation

<u>Interfaces</u>:

- RS232C
- Cash drawer
- Ethernet
- Wireless LAN
- USB

<u>Load cell</u>:

The load cell is a CAS load cell, model TPN, capacities as per following table.
