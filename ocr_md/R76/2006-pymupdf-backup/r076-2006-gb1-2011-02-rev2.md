        −  LCD or LED display
        −   Functions keys
        −   Connections and ports located at the back

Load cell:

Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:
         −   There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load

         −   The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data

                particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only  if
              humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.

         −   The  compatibility  of  the  load  cells and  indicator  is  established by  the
              manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules calculation at the time of
                 verification.
         −   The load cell transmission conforms to a standard type.
