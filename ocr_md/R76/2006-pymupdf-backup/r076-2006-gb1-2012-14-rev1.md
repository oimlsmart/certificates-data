Characteristics of the instrument:

Characteristics:

The  indicating  devices  are  designated  the DD1010, DD1010I, DD1010IC, DD1010H,
DD1010IH, and DD1010ICH indicators. The indicators are self-indicating, mains or DC-
powered, and are designed to be used as part of a Class III or IIII, non-automatic weighing
instrument.
