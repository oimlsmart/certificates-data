Identification of the certified pattern

General description
UNI-9 is a single or multi-interval non-automatic weighing and price-computing instrument with
label/receipt printer. It is approved for direct sales to the public or for price labelling.

Essential characteristics
-  Determination of stability of equilibrium
-  Zero indicator, (indicating zero within +- 0,25 e) mandatory
-  Semi-automatic zero-setting
-   Initial zero-setting
-  Zero-tracking (not mandatory)
-  Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing
-  Preset tare
-  Adjustment / gravity set-up mode via a switch on the A/D board
-  Checking the display
-  Price calculation
-  Level indicator and adjustable feet’s used for levelling
-  Indications other than primary indications
-  Price labelling instrument
-  PLU function
