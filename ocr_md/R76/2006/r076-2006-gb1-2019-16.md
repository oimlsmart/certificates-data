<b>DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX</b>

<b><u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u></b>

The Cargo platform scales, comprising an Atrax CDI-1600 indicator connected to a weighnig platform, form a family of Class III, self-indicating, single-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<b><u>Construction:</u></b>

The indicator has the following features:

– Stainless steel enclosure fitted with panel mount bracket or tilt stand\
– Colour LCD display\
– 27 button keypad (5 function keys, 5 primary scale function keys, 4 navigation keys, enter key, 12-key numeric keypad)\
– Connections and ports located on the bottom face

The weighing platform has the following features:

– Mild steel construction\
– CRF (Cargo Roller Frame): frame consisting of rollers or conveyor belt mounted on SBM-2T mounts under the four corners. Loads are transfered on and off the platofmr after static weighing only.\
– CPF (Cargo Platform Frame): frame with or without rollers mounted on top, with the SBM-5T mounts under the four corners. Loads are transfered on and off the platofmr after static weighing only.\
– CVF (Cargo Vechile Frame): pit mounted heavy duty plaf orm, consisting of weigh beam modules housing the DSB-25klb mounts and connecting modules which can be four load mounts (2 x beams and 2 x modules) or six load mounts (3 x beams and 3 x modules).\
– <i>[yellow-highlighted comment marker]</i> Load cells manufactured by AnyLoad (see table below for number and capacities.)\
– Size accodring to the table below.

<b><u>Devices:</u></b>

– Semi-automatic zero setting (≤ 4% of Max)\
– Zero tracking (≤ 4% of Max)\
– Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)\
– Preset Tare\
– Zero indicator\
– Indication of stable equilibrium\
– Gross/Net/Tare display\
– Single or dual-scale\
– Totalisation\
– Display check at power up\
– Printing\
– Data storage device (Alibi memory)

<b><u>Software:</u></b>

The legally relevant software is designated version V2.xx.xx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes. This information can be displayed by selecting Menu/Supervisor/About, or when the indicator is powered on during display of the information screen.
