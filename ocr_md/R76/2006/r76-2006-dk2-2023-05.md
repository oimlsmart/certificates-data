## Descriptive annex

**Characteristics CI-10xA Series**

- Accuracy class&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<strong>III</strong>
- Single interval, multi interval (dual)
- Maximum number of verification scale intervals: 3000 or 2x3000
- Maximum capacity (Max): 60 to 600 kg
- Minimum capacity (Min): 20 × e<sub>i</sub>
- Verification scale interval(e<sub>i</sub>): Max<sub>i</sub>/ n<sub>i</sub>
- Minimum input voltage per VSI: 1.2 μV/e
- Excitation voltage: 3.3 VDC
- Minimum input impedance: 350 ohm
- Maximum input impedance: 1000 ohm
- Power supply: 5.9 VDC from external mains adapter for 100-240VAC<br>Optional 4.8 VDC rechargeable battery<br>Optional dry cell batteries, 4x1.5VDC
- Maximum tare effect: -Max or -Max<sub>1</sub>
- Temperature range. -10 °C to +40 °C

**Software**

The software is designated “V1.xx”.<br>
This information is displayed at power up and can be displayed in one of the following ways:<br>
“V1.xx” or “u1.xx” or “U1.xx” or<br>
“V 1.xx” or “u 1.xx” or “U.1.xx” or<br>
“V1xx” or “u1xx” or “U1xx” or<br>
“v 1xx” or “u 1xx” or “u 1xx”<br><br>
V can be displayed as u or U on a segment display.<br>
xx represents minor non-legal changes and can be number, characters, symbol or left blank.

**Devices**

- Initial zero setting device on power up (≤ 20% Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting (≤ 4% Max)
- Automatic zero-setting (requires stable below zero for at least 5 sec.)
- Zero tracking (optional) (≤ 4% Max)
- Zero enunciator
- Net enunciators
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing
- Gross/Net toggle
- Indication of stable equilibrium
- Gravity compensation
- Printing (though I/O)
