**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument</u>:

This family of instruments, designated the CI-600 Series, utilises the CI-601A, CI-605A or CI-607A digital indicating devices connected to a weighing platform to form a single or dual-interval, Class III or IIII, non-automatic weighing instrument.

The instruments are designated the CI-601A and CI-605A respectively, and are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Main features</u>:

- Plastic enclosure
- 4.3" LCD display
- Operator keypad with numerical, navigation and function keys
- Relay In/Out(4 inputs, 6 outputs) option card (CI-605A)
- Relay In/Out(6 inputs, 8 outputs) option card (CI-607A)
- Analogue Out option card (CI-601A, CI-605A, CI-607A)

<u>Devices</u>:

- Initial zero setting device on power up (≤ 20% Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting (≤ 4% Max)
- Zero tracking (optional) (≤ 4% Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = -Max)
- Preset tare
- Gross/Net indication
- Zero-indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium
- Net indicator
- Gravity compensation
- Hold function
- Soft functions keys (F1, F2 and F3 can be allocated a function)
- Memory storage

<u>Interfaces</u>:

- Load cell connection
- RS232/485
- Relay In/Out (4 inputs, 6 outputs) (CI-605A)
- Relay In/Out (6 inputs, 8 outputs) (CI-607A)
- Analogue Out (CI-601A, CI-605A, CI-607A)

<u>Load cell</u>:

Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:

- There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load cell.
- The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer’s declaration of compatibility of modules, and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.
- The compatibility of the load cells and indicator is established by the manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules calculation at the time of verification.
- The load cell transmission conforms to a standard type.

<u>Software</u>:

The software is held in firmware on the circuit board, and has the identification number “V1.xx”, with xx reflecting non-legally relevant changes. The software version number is displayed at power-up.

Download of software is only possible by accessing the main board inside the sealed enclosure. Access to the legally relevant parameters is prevented by a jumper on the main board.

<u>Sealing</u>:

Access to the electronics, access to the switch described in Software section and the load cell connection are sealed using a tamper-evident method.

<u>Alternatives</u>:

Having a modified instrument designed to be connected to a digital load cell, and designated as follows:

- CI-601D when fitted with Analogue Out card
- CI-605D when fitted with Analogue Out and Relay In/Out (4 inputs, 6 outputs) card
- CI-607D when fitted with Analogue Out and Relay In/Out (6 inputs, 8 outputs) card

The digital load cell may be a WBK-D, manufactured by CAS Corporation, as described in OIML Certificate R60/2000-NL1.1308.
