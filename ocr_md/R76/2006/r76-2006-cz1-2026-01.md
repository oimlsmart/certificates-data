## 1 Characteristics of the instrument

The electronic indicator type MZ3042 is intended for use as indicator for non-automatic weighing instruments for medical applications. The instrument is a Class III, self-indicating weighing instrument with single- or dual-range, an external AC mains adapter and an internal rechargeable battery (optional).

## 3 Devices and functions

- determination of stability of equilibrium

- indication of stable equilibrium

- zero indicating

- initial zero setting $ \leq 20\% $ Max

- zero tracking $ \leq 4 \% $ Max

- semi-automatic zero setting $ \leq 4 \% $ Max

- semi-automatic tare balancing (subtractive)

- Preset tare device

- Hold function

## 3.1 Additional Instrument Functions

In addition to its primary weighing function, the instrument incorporates supplementary features including:

- Body Mass Index (BMI) calculation

- Length measurement

- Print function

These additional functions have not been tested for compliance.
