## Descriptive annex

**Characteristics**

<table>
  <tr><td>Type:</td><td>LCA-2</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Accuracy class:</td><td>III and IIII</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Weighing range:</td><td>Single-interval, multi-range /Tripple) or multi-interval (Tripple)</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Maximum number of Verification Scale Intervals:</td><td>10000 (class III), 1000 (class IIII)</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Maximum tare effect:</td><td>-Max</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Fractional factor:</td><td>p'i = 0.5</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Minimum input-voltage per VSI:</td><td>0.5 µV</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Minimum signal voltage for dead load:</td><td>0 mV</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Excitation voltage:</td><td>10 VDC nominal</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Circuit for remote sense:</td><td>Active</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Minimum input-impedance:</td><td>44 ohm</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Maximum input-impedance:</td><td>1100 ohm</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Mains power supply:</td><td>12-24 VDC, or<br>230 VAC (50/60 Hz) using external AC/DC adapter</td></tr>
</table>

**Software**

The software revision level is displayed during the power up sequence of the instrument.<br>
The approved software version is: 1.x.y<br>
x and y are subversion numbers for error correction and minor changes of non-legal functionality.

**Devices**

- Semi-automatic zero-setting device, range ±2% of Max
- Automatic zero-tracking device, range ±2% of Max
- Initial zero-setting device, range±10% of Max
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare device
- Printing
- Operator information messages
- Zero, Stable equilibrium and Net indicators.

**Interfaces**

- RS-232 / RS485C
- RS422

The interfaces do not have to be secured.
