**Software**

The software version may be viewed by sending “FFV” to the unit, which responds with ‘Vxx.yy’ or ‘V:xx.yy.zz’.

where xx denotes the legally relevant code, yy denotes the major non-legally relevant code, and zz denotes the minor non-legally relevant code.

The tested software version is: V01.60

The software changes from V01.60 to V02.00.00 have been examined.

**Devices**

- Initial zero-setting (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare
- Preset tare
- Automatic tare
- Zero detection
- Stable weight detection
- Event counter (TAC)
