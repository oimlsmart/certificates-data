**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The UDS-series is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<u>Device:</u>

- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- Serial data interface RS232C
- ZigBee
- Bluetooth

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version **V 1.xx**, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing</u>:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The enclosure is secured by a lead and wire type seal through securing screws.
