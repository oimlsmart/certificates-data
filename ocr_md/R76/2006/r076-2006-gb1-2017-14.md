**Characteristics of the instrument:**

The Dolphin Series is a family of Class III, mains or battery-operated, self-indicating, dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. It comprises the EB, DBII and DB-1H models and their variants (Figures 1 to 4), using the One-Module V4.

The instrument may be used for direct sales to the public.

The software identification shall be V4.xx.x, with xx.x reflecting non-legally relevant changes. This information is displayed at power up.
