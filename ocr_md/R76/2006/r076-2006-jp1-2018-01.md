## DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The SJ-AWP/SJ-AWP-BT model is a class III, self-indicating, multi-interval instruments non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Device:</u>

- Semi-automatic zero-setting (≤4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tera weighing (T = - Max)
- Preset tare
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- Bluetooth interface (SJ-..AWP-BT only)

<u>Software:</u>

The legally relevant software is designated version P-1.xx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper-evident lead and wire type seal.
