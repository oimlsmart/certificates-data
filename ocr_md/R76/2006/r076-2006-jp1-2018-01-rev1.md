**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The SJ-..WP, SJ-..WP-BT, SJ-..AWP and SJ-..AWP-BT models is a class III, self-indicating, multi-interval instruments non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<table border="1">
<tr>
<td>Type</td>
<td align="center">SJ-3000WP<br>SJ-3000WP-BT<br>SJ-3000AWP<br>SJ-3000AWP-BT</td>
<td align="center">SJ-6000WP<br>SJ-6000WP-BT<br>SJ-6000AWP<br>SJ-6000AWP-BT</td>
<td align="center">SJ-15KWP<br>SJ-15KWP-BT<br>SJ-15KAWP<br>SJ-15KAWP-BT</td>
<td align="center">SJ-30KWP<br>SJ-30KWP-BT<br>SJ-30KAWP<br>SJ-30KAWP-BT</td>
</tr>
<tr><td>Class</td><td colspan="4" align="center">III</td></tr>
<tr><td>Max</td><td align="center">1500/3000 g</td><td align="center">3000/6000 g</td><td align="center">6/15 kg</td><td align="center">15/30 kg</td></tr>
<tr><td>e</td><td align="center">0.5/1 g</td><td align="center">1/2 g</td><td align="center">0.002/0.005 kg</td><td align="center">0.005/0.01 kg</td></tr>

[Watermark: OIML Certification System]

<u>Device:</u>

- Semi-automatic zero-setting (≤4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
- Preset tare
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- Bluetooth interface (SJ-..WP-BT and SJ-..AWP-BT only)

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version P-1.xx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper-evident lead and wire type seal.
