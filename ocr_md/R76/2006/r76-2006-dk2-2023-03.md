- Minimum capacity (Min):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;20 × e

- Label Printing
- Stable equilibrium, Zero and Net indicators.
- PLU tables
- Totalization

- RS232C
- Cash Drawer
- Etherner
- Wireless LAN
- USB
