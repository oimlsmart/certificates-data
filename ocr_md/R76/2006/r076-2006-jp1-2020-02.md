# DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;The J-101W is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

  <tr>

    <td colspan="3" align="center">J-101W</td>

<p>[Diagonal watermark: "OIML Certification System"]</p>

<u>Device:</u>

<ul>
  <li>Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20% of Max)</li>
  <li>Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)</li>
  <li>Zero-tracking (≤4% of Max)</li>
  <li>Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)</li>
  <li>Zero indicator</li>
  <li>Indication of stable equilibrium device</li>
</ul>

<u>Interfaces:</u>

<ul>
  <li>ZigBee</li>
  <li>Bluetooth</li>
</ul>

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;The software is designated version V 1.15.

<u>Sealing:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;Access to the calibration switch is prevented by a temper-evident lead and wire type seal.
