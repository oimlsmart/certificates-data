**Software**

The version of the software is displayed during the power-up sequence of the instrument.  
The approved software version is 2.x.yyy.  
x and yyy are subversion numbers for error correction and minor changes of non-legal functionality.

**Devices**

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing device
- Units (Allowed units are g, kg and t.)
- Stable equilibrium, Zero and Net indicators.
- Extended indication.
