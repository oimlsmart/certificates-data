**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The B Series comprises a BPS indicator (model) connected to a range of load cells to form a family of Class III, mains or battery-operated, self-indicating, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. The BPS indicator may be designated as the BPS and their variants, BRS and BLS models depending upon the construction.

Max ≤ 30 kg  
Min ≥ 40 g  
e ≥ 2 g  
n ≤ 6000 (for single-interval)  
ni ≤ 3000 (for dual-interval)  

The instrument may be used for direct sales to the public.

<u>Construction:</u>

- Plastic enclosure
- Plastic / Stainless Steel load receptor
- Dual, integral or pole-mounted, LCD/LED display

- Level indicator

<u>Devices:</u>

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking (optional) (≤ 4% Max)

<u>Load cell:</u>

Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:

- There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load cell.
- The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer’s declaration of compatibility of modules, and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.
- The compatibility of the load cells and indicator is established by the manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules calculation at the time of verification.
- The load cell transmission conforms to a standard type.

<u>Software:</u>

The software is held in firmware on the circuit board, and has the identification number V02.xx.xx, with xx.xx reflecting non-legally relevant changes. The software version number is displayed at power-up.

Access to the legally relevant parameters and download of software is only possible via the calibration switch on the main board.

<u>Interfaces</u>

The instrument may have the following interface types:

- USB
- Ethernet
- RS232

The instruments may be connected to an Electronic Point of Sale (EPOS), Electronic Cash Register (ECR) or Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT/ECU).

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the electronics, load cell and calibration switch is prevented by sealing the enclosure using a tamper-evident method – wire and seal solution.

<u>Alternatives:</u>

There are currently no authorised alternatives.
