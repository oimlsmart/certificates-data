**Characteristics of the instrument:**

<u>**Characteristics:**</u>

The instrument, designated the CL7200-D Series, Class III, mains-powered, self-indicating, price-computing, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instrument is designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Main features:</u>

- Plastic construction
- Operator's keypad
- Stainless steel load receptor
- Operator front touch screen display and customer rear LCD display
- Level indicator under the load receptor
- Integrated printer

<u>Devices:</u>

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Net indicator
- Stable weight indicator
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Preset tare
- Gravity compensation
- Price-computing
- Totalisation (including non-weighed items)
- PLU
- Label printing
- Fixed weight labelling
- Multi-vendor operation
- Self-service operation
- Modular operator/customer displays
- Calibration / set-up mode via sealed internal switch

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- RS232C
- PS/2
- Cash drawer
- Ethernet
- Wireless LAN
- USB
- HDMI
- Audio out

The instrument may be fitted with a **CAS WI-FI Module, model GWF-3M08**.
