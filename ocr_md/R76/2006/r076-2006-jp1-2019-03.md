## DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX

Characteristics of the instrument:

The SB...series is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

## Device:

- Semi-automatic zero-setting device ( $ \leq $ 4% of Max)

- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device

- Zero indicator

- Indication of stable equilibrium device
