## DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX

Characteristics of the instrument:

The ALG-H series are class I and class II, self-indicating and non-automatic weighing instruments.

The mass detection method of the ALG-H series is a tuning fork sensor.

The instruments can be switched to the unit of measurement (momme) for special

applications, e.g. trade with pearls.The symbol for the momme is mom(1 mom = 3.75 g).

## Device:

- Initial zero-setting device ( $ \leq $ 20 % of Max)

- Semi-automatic zero-setting device ( $ \leq $ 4 % of Max)

- Zero-tracking ( $ \leq $ 4 % of Max)

- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)

- Indication of stable equilibrium device

## Interface:

- RS232C (to connect personal computer, printer and external display etc.)

- USB interface (to connect Personal computer and mobile battery)

- One of the following interfaces may be incorporated:

- RS232C (to connect personal computer, printer and external display etc.)

- Relay output(to connect indicator)

- Ethernet (to connect personal computer)

- Bluetooth (to connect personal computer)

## Software:

The legally relevant software is designated version A28B.
