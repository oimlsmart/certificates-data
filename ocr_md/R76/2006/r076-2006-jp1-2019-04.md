**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument</u>:

The GX-A series is a class II, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data</u>:

<table>
  <tr><td>Type</td><td colspan="3" align="center">GX-A series</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Class</td><td colspan="3" align="center">II</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Max</td><td align="center">122g to 620g</td><td align="center">1220g to 6200g</td><td align="center">6200g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>e</td><td align="center">0.01g</td><td align="center">0.1g</td><td align="center">1g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>d</td><td align="center">0.001g</td><td align="center">0.01g</td><td align="center">0.1g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Min</td><td align="center">0.02g</td><td align="center">0.5g</td><td align="center">5g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Temperature range</td><td colspan="3" align="center">+ 10 to + 30 °C</td></tr>
</table>

<u>Device</u>:

- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tera weighing (T = - Max)
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces</u>:

- Serial data interface RS232C (to connect Printer)
- USB interface (to connect Personal computer)

<u>Software</u>:

The legally relevant software is designated version P-1.xxx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing</u>:

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper evident seals.
