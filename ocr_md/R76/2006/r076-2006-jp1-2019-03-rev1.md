**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The SB...series is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<table>
  <tr><td>Type</td><td>SB...series</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Class</td><td>III</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Max</td><td>255 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>e</td><td>0.1 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Min</td><td>2 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Temperature range</td><td>+ 5 to + 40 °C</td></tr>
</table>

[Watermark: OIML Certification System]

<u>Device:</u>

- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version V.xx.01.xx (Scale BOX) and V.xx.03.xx (Terminal), with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper-evident seal on the base of the instrument.
