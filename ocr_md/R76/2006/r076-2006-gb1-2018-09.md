**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The instrument, designated the CL3000 Series, Class III, mains-powered, self-indicating, price-computing, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instrument is designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Main features:</u>

- Plastic construction
- Operator's keypad
- Stainless steel load receptor
- Front and rear LCD displays (CL3000-B)
- Pole-mounted double-sided LCD display (CL3000-P)
- Level indicator under the load receptor
- Integrated printer

<u>Devices:</u>

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Net indicator
- Stable weight indicator
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Preset tare
- Gravity compensation
- Price-computing
- Totalisation (including non-weighed items)
- PLU
- Fixed weight labelling
- Multi-vendor operation
- Calibration / set-up mode via sealed internal switch
- Self-service operation

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- RS232C
- Cash drawer
- Ethernet
- Wireless LAN
- USB

<u>Load cell:</u>

The instrument is fitted with one CAS load cell, model TPN, E<sub>max</sub> as per following table.

<u>Software:</u>

The software identification shall be V3.xx.x, with xx.x reflecting minor, non-legally relevant modifications. This information is displayed at power up.
Access to the legally relevant parameters and download of software is only possible via a calibration switch on the main board.

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper-evident seal on the base of the instrument.
