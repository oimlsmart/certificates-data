## Characteristics of the instrument:

The instrument is a CI-200 Series, Class III, mains or battery-operated, self-indicating, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instrument. It consists of a CI-200 Series indicator connected to a weighing platform. The CI-200 Series comprises the CI-200A, CI-201A, CI-200S and CI-200SC models.

The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Main features</u>:

  – Plastic (CI-200A and CI-201A) or stainless steel (CI-200S and CI-200SC) enclosure  
  – LED (CI-200A, CI-200S and CI-200SC) or LCD (CI-201A) display  
  – LED indicators (CI-200A, CI-200S and CI-200SC)  
  – LCD indicators (CI-201A)  
  – Alphanumerical keypad  
  – Battery indicators (low, charging)

<u>Devices</u>:

  – Initial zero setting device on power up  
  – Semi-automatic zero setting  
  – Zero tracking (optional)  
  – Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing  
  – Zero-indicator  
  – Indication of stable equilibrium  
  – Net indicator  
  – Gravity compensation  
  – Printing  
  – Hold function  
  – Counting mode (CI-201A)  
  – Percent mode (CI-201A)  
  – Totalisation (CI-201A)  
  – Checkweighing (CI-201A and CI-200SC)

<u>Interfaces</u>:

  – Load cell connection  
  – RS232/485  
  – USB

<u>Load cell</u>:

Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:

  – There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load cell.  
  – The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer's declaration of compatibility of modules, and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.  
  – The compatibility of the load cells and indicator is established by the manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules calculation at the time of verification.  
  – The load cell transmission conforms to a standard type.

<u>Software</u>:

The software is held in firmware on the circuit board, and has the identification number “V1.xx” or “V2.xx”, with xx reflecting non-legally relevant changes. The software version number is displayed at power-up.

Download of software is only possible by accessing the main board inside the sealed enclosure.

Access to the legally relevant parameters is prevented by a switch on the main board.

<u>Sealing</u>:

Access to the electronics, access to the switch described in Software section and the load cell connection are sealed using a tamper-evident method
