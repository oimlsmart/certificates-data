**Software**

The legally relevant software has version 1.0.xx, where xx can be 04 to 99 reflecting non-legally relevant changes.
The software version is displayed as part of the power-up sequence.

**Interfaces**

- RS232

**Devices**

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare device
- Gross / Net display
- Printing device
- Weighing unit toggling device
- Gravity compensation device
- Stable equilibrium, Zero AND Net indicators.
- Calibration event counter
- Configuration event counter
