## Software

The 195/225 indicator software is segregated into parts, where the legally relevant part is the software that includes boot loader, standard indicator and streaming of weighing results to RS232 or Bluetooth, while the application software apart from display of the weight (see Section 5.2 and 6.2) shall be non-legally relevant in order to be covered by this type approval certificate.

The software revision level is displayed during the power up sequence of the instrument as XX.YY.ZZZ where XX is the version of the legally relevant software and YY.ZZZ is the subversion not affecting the legally functions of the software part.
