**Descriptive annex**

**Characteristics**

- Accuracy class&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;III
- Single interval, multi interval (2 partial intervals)
- Maximum number of verification scale intervals:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3000 or 2x3000
- Maximum capacity (Max):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3 kg to 30 kg
- Minimum capacity (Min):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;20 × e
- Verification scale interval(e):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≥ 1 g
- Maximum tare effect:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -Max or ≤ -Max<sub>1</sub>
- Temperature range.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-10 °C to +40 °C

Power supply:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;100-240 VAC to 9 VDC using external<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;adapter or<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4 size D 1.5V dry batteries

[Watermark: CS OIML Certification System]

**Model variants and designation**

The DSW-100C has a 6-digit LCD display with appropriate indicators.<br>
The DSW-100E has two pcs. o the same display – one in the front and one in the rear.

The load cell used in the instruments is a DB-1 C3 with v<sub>min</sub> ≤ e and 0.6×E<sub>max</sub> ≤ Max ≤ 0.9×E<sub>max</sub>. from Shanghai Digital Balance Electronic Co., Ltd.

**Software**

The software version is:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3.53

This information is displayed at power up.

**Sealing**

Access to the configuration and calibration facility is achieved by pressing a calibration switch accessed through a hole in the bottom of the enclosure of the scale.

&nbsp;Sealing of the access to the calibration switch, electronics and connection of the load cell is accomplished by a plate covering access to the switch.

The cover is sealed with wire and seal which at the same time seal the access to the calibration switch and the inside of the enclosure containing the electronics and the connection of the load cell to the electronics.
