**Identification of the pattern (continued)**

Non-automatic electromechanical weighing instrument performed as multi-interval and multiple range instrument.

The weighing ranges with Max, Min, e, d and number of verification scale intervals may be chosen in the limits of No. 3.2 of R 76-1 and considering the limiting values of the weighing modules.

Environmental conditions and influence factors:

Climatic: Temperature range: +10 °C to 30 °C
Humidity: up to 85 % at 40 °C, not condensing
Mechanical: not applicable
Electromagnetic: OIML R76 (2006) except B.3.7
