**Characteristics**

Type:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ProCuity MedSurg Bed  
Accuracy Class:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IIII  
Weighing ranges:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Single-interval  
Maximum capacity (Max):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;250 kg  
Minimum capacity (Min):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;20 kg  
Verification scale interval (e=):&nbsp;&nbsp;2 kg  
Maximum numbers of  
verification scale intervals&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;n=125  
Maximum tare effect:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- 60 kg  
Operating temperature range:&nbsp;&nbsp;5°C to 35°C  
Power supply:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;100 – 240 VAC, 50/60 Hz

## Software

The legal software for the scale has its own version and checksum.  
These can be found in the setting menu.

Allowed version are  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v01.08.000.01 with checksum 0xE86A93DA  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v01.09.000.01 with checksum 0x77DAA10D  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;v01.10.000.01 with checksum 0x22920465

## Devices

-   Semi-automatic subtractive tare device
-   Exchange equipment function*)
-   Weight logging device
-   Operator information messages
-   Gravity compensation device (sealed)
-   Stable equilibrium and Net indicators.

\*) Lock the present net weight and take any change in the gross weight as a change in tare weight.

## Interfaces

The scale has no interfaces.

## Load cells

4 pcs. K-TSTB/300LBSTR1-1 manufactured by HBM, Stryker P/N 300900370057
