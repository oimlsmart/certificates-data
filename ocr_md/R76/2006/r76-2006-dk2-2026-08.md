**Software**

The scales have software separation. The legal relevant software version is 1.11 or 2.00, and application software have version 1.xx(y)(z), where xx can be 00..99, where (y) can be 0..9 or blank, and where (z) can be a..z or blank.
The application software version is displayed during the power-up sequence of the instrument.
The legal relevant software version can be displayed by pressing the “TARE” key during the power-up sequence of the instrument.

**Devices**

- Zero-setting range: ±2 % of Max.
- Automatic zero-tracking range: ±2 % of Max.
- Initial zero-setting range: ±10 % of Max.
- Semi-automatic zero-setting
- Zero-tracking
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare
- Semi-automatic tare
- Preset Tare (only ZHC… / UTC…)
- Extended resolution (only ZHW… / ZHC… / UTW… / UTC… / ZTW…)
- Price look up table (PLU)
- Hold unit price
- Non-weighed articles
- Totalization (only WTP… / WSP… / UTP… / USP…)
- Check Weighing device
- Percentage weighing
- Printing (external printer connected to interface)
- Counting device
- Gravity compensation
- Unit g or kg

**Interfaces**

- RS-232 interface.
