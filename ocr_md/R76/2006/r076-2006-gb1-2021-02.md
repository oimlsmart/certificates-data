<strong>DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX</strong>

<u><strong>Characteristics of the instrument:</strong></u>

The Flintec M300 Infant Scale is a Class III, mains or battery-powered, self-indicating, Non-automatic Weighing Instrument, and is designed to record the weight of a baby.

The instrument shall not be used for direct sales to the public.

<u><strong>Metrological characteristics:</strong></u>

<table>
  <tr><td align="center">Max</td><td align="center">Min</td><td align="center">e =</td></tr>
  <tr><td align="center">6/15 kg</td><td align="center">0.040 kg</td><td align="center">2/5 g</td></tr>
</table>

<u><strong>Construction:</strong></u>

– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;LCD display fitted into load receptor: 5 digits, with Zero, Net and Hold indicators.  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Three buttons (Tare, Hold, and On/Off/Zero)  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Aluminium base enclosure containing the load cell and electronics  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Plastic load receptor with tray for baby weighing  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Level indicator next to the display  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Four adjustable feet for levelling  

<u><strong>Devices:</strong></u>

– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Zero-tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Zero indicator.  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing device  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Net indicator.  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hold facility.  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hold indicator.  
– &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stable indicator  

<u><strong>Load cell:</strong></u>

The load cells are based on Flintec planar beam PB7.5 kg. These are configured to enable use with the scales electronics which utilises the Acam Picostrain measuring principle.

<u><strong>Rated operation conditions:</strong></u>

The instrument may be powered from 2 x 1.5 V internal batteries or from a 12 V DC 300 mA, mains adaptor. Any compatible conformity assessed and marked mains adaptor may be used. The weight indication will be replaced with 'lo bAt' when the battery voltage falls below 2.14 V DC. The instrument switches off when the mains adaptor voltage falls below 5.6 V DC. The On/Off/Zero button needs to be pressed again to turn the scale on again under battery power.

The temperature range for the instrument is +5 °C to +35 °C.

<u><strong>Software:</strong></u>

The software is designated V1.03, this information is displayed when the Hold button is pressed for more than 8 seconds.

<u><strong>Interfaces:</strong></u>

The instrument has the following interface type:

– &nbsp;&nbsp;DC voltage input

<u><strong>Sealing:</strong></u>

The instrument is sealed by placing a tamper-evident label over one of the screws in the base plate which prevents opening of the housing. Access to the service button that allows calibration is also prevented by placing a tamper-evident label over the access hole.

<u><strong>Alternatives:</strong></u>

There are currently no authorised alternatives.
