**Descriptive annex**

**Characteristics**

- Accuracy class&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;III
- Single interval, multi interval (dual)
- Maximum number of verification scale intervals:&nbsp;&nbsp;3000 or 2x3000
- Maximum capacity (Max):&nbsp;&nbsp;6 kg to 30 kg
- Minimum capacity (Min):&nbsp;&nbsp;20 × e
- Verification scale interval(e):&nbsp;&nbsp;≥ 1 g
- Maximum tare effect:&nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -2.999 kg or
  &nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -2.998 kg or
  &nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -5.998 kg or
  &nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -5.995 kg or
  &nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -9.995 kg or
  &nbsp;&nbsp;≤ -14.99 kg
- Temperature range.&nbsp;&nbsp;-10 °C to +40 °C
- Power supply:&nbsp;&nbsp;100-240 VAC 50/60Hz

**Software**

The software is designated “YY V3.xx.xx.<em>ZZZZZ</em>”, where

- &nbsp;xx.xx is reflecting non-legally relevant changes and may be numbers, letters, symbols or blank,
- &nbsp;YY is a 2-digit country code
- &nbsp;<em>ZZZZZ</em> is a function code and may be numbers, letters, symbols or blank

This information is displayed at power up and in the menu.

Access to the legally relevant parameters and download of software is only possible by accessing the calibration switch on the main board.

**Sealing**

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by sealing of the enclosure by wire and seal or a tamperproof sticker.

**Devices**

- &nbsp;Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- &nbsp;Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- &nbsp;Automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- &nbsp;Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- &nbsp;Zero indicator
- &nbsp;Net indicator
- &nbsp;Stable weight indicator
- &nbsp;Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- &nbsp;Preset tare
- &nbsp;Gravity compensation
- &nbsp;Price-computing
- &nbsp;Totalisation (including non-weighed items)
- &nbsp;PLU
- &nbsp;Fixed weight labelling
- &nbsp;Multi-vendor operation
- &nbsp;Self-service operation
- &nbsp;Calibration / set-up mode via sealed internal switch
