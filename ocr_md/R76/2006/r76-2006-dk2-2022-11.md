**Descriptive annex**

**Characteristics**

-   Accuracy class&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;III
-   Single interval, multi.interval (dual), multirange (dual)
-   Maximum number of verification scale intervals:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≤5000
-   Maximum tare effect:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-Max
-   Fraction factor&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;p'I = 0.5
-   Minimum input voltage per VSI:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≥1 µV
-   Excitation voltage:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;5 VDC
-   Circuit for remote sense:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;present on the model with 7- and 8-terminal connector
-   Minimum input impedance:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;87 ohm
-   Maximum input impedance:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1100 ohm
-   Maximum cable length to junction box:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;89 m/mm²
-   Temperature range.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-10 °C to +40 °C
-   Power supply:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;100-240VAC 50/60 Hz.
    6 VDC rechargeable battery

*[Watermark: OIML Certification System logo — large, gray, overlaid across the page]*

**Software**

The approved software is version VER-01
This information is displayed at power up.

**Devices**

-   Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
-   Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
-   Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
-   Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
-   Printing
-   Stable equilibrium, Zero and Net indicators.
-   Totalization
-   Counting
-   Option for battery operation
