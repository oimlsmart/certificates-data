**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The DP-6601 is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<table>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>Type</td><td colspan="3" align="center">DP-6601</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>Class</td><td colspan="3" align="center">III</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>Max</td><td align="center">3000 g</td><td align="center">6000 g</td><td align="center">15000 g</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>e</td><td align="center">1 g</td><td align="center">2 g</td><td align="center">5 g</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>Min</td><td align="center">20 g</td><td align="center">40 g</td><td align="center">100 g</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  <tr><td>Temperature range</td><td colspan="3" align="center">0 °C to + 40 °C</td></tr>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</table>

<u>Device:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20 % of Max)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4 % of Max)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Zero-tracking (≤ 4 % of Max)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Automatic tare device
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Zero indicator
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- ZigBee
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- Blutooth

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version V 1.xx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The enclosure is secured by a lead and wire type seal through securing screws.
