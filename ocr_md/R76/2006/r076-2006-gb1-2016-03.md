## Characteristics of the instrument:

<u>Characteristics</u>:

The indicating device is designated the Avery Weigh-Tronix ZM510-SD4. The indicator is programmable, self-indicating, mains-powered, and is designed to be used as part of a Class III or IIII, Non-Automatic Weighing Instrument.

<u>Construction</u>:

The indicator construction is dependent on the model number, the designation follows the following format: “Prefix-XYZ”, with

- Model Number Prefix:
  ZM510 = Standard Indicator

- First Digit X – Enclosure material
  S = Stainless enclosure

- Second Digit Y – Mounting orientation
  D = Desktop

- Third Digit Z – Display Type
  4 = Dot graphic – Green background with Black Dots

  The ZM510-SD4 features 48 operational keys, including a QWERTY keypad and 5 “soft keys” (F1 to F5), the current functions of which are shown above them on the display.

<u>Devices</u>:

- Semi-automatic zero setting (≤ 4% Max)
- Zero tracking (≤ 4% Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing
- Pre-set tare
- Recall of Gross indication when tare is active
- Determination of stability of equilibrium
- Indication of stability of equilibrium
- Checking of display
- Printing
- PLUs
- Alibi storage device
- Gravity compensation
- Real time clock
- Command via external device (PC)
- Gross, Net, Tare, Preset tare, Print, Zero, Motion, Accumulation, Over/Under weight, Network and Battery indicators.
- Single or multi-range / multi-interval (maximum of 3 partial ranges)
- Range in use indicators (multi-range variant)
- Connection to up to 4 load receptors, with load receptor number indicator
- Connection to digital load cells
