## Characteristics of the instrument:

This family of instruments is designated the CL5500 Series, and comprises the CL3500-B and CL3500-P models. The instruments are Class III, mains-operated, self-indicating, pricecomputing, single or dual-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments. The instruments are designed for direct sales to the public.
