**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The SB…series is a class Ⅲ, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<table>
<tr><td>Type</td><td align="center">SB…series</td></tr>
<tr><td>Class</td><td align="center">Ⅲ</td></tr>
<tr><td>Max</td><td align="center">255 kg</td></tr>
<tr><td>e</td><td align="center">0.1 kg</td></tr>
<tr><td>Min</td><td align="center">2 kg</td></tr>
<tr><td>Temperature range</td><td align="center">+ 5 °C to + 40 °C</td></tr>
</table>

<u>Device:</u>

- **Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)**
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

<u>Software:</u>

The legally relevant software is designated version V.xx.01.xx (Scale BOX) and V.xx.03.xx (Terminal), with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper-evident seal on the base of the instrument.
