**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The instruments considered here are self-indicating, single-interval, non-automatic weighing instruments designed to be used in paint mixing applications. The family comprises of the PMA-EV (Class II) and the PMA-HD (Class III) types, each of which comprises several models that are listed below.

The instrument shall not be used for direct sales to the public.

<u>Load cell:</u>

The instrument is fitted with one strain gauge load cell as per technical data, manufactured by Bizerba.

<u>Technical data:</u>

The instrument operates on a 5 V DC power supply provided by USB. For models LAB1X\*, EVO1X\*, VIS1X\*, VIS1Y\*, power is delivered from USB to Ex-link converter, and then to the instrument via RJ-45.
