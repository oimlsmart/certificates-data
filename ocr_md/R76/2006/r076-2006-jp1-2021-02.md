**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The GX-M series are class Ⅰ and class Ⅱ, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.

The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<table>
  <tr><td>Type</td><td>GX-8202M</td><td>GX-10202M</td><td>GX-12001M</td><td>GX-22001M</td><td>GX-32001M</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Class</td><td>Ⅰ</td><td>Ⅰ</td><td>Ⅱ</td><td>Ⅱ</td><td>Ⅱ</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Max</td><td>8.2 kg</td><td>10.2 kg</td><td>12.2 kg</td><td>22.2 kg</td><td>32.2 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>e</td><td>0.1 g</td><td>0.1 g</td><td>1 g</td><td>1 g</td><td>1 g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>d</td><td>0.01 g</td><td>0.01 g</td><td>0.1 g</td><td>0.1 g</td><td>0.1 g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Min</td><td>1 g</td><td>1 g</td><td>5 g</td><td>5 g</td><td>5 g</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Temperature range</td><td colspan="5">+ 10 °C to + 30 °C</td></tr>
</table>

<u>Device:</u>

- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- USB interface (to connect Personal computer)
- Serial data interface RS232C (to connect Printer)

<u>Software:</u>

The legally relevant software is designated version P1.XXX, with X reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by a tamper evident seals.
