<p align="right"><strong>OIML Certificate No.<br>R76/2006-A-JP1-25.01</strong></p>

<p><strong>DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX</strong></p>

<p><u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u></p>

<p>The InBody770S is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.<br>
The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.</p>

<p><u>Technical data:</u></p>

<table border="1">
  <tr>
    <td>Type</td>
    <td align="center">InBody770S</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Class</td>
    <td align="center">III</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Max</td>
    <td align="center">270 kg</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>e</td>
    <td align="center">0.1 kg</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Min</td>
    <td align="center">2 kg</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Temperature range</td>
    <td align="center">+ 10 °C to + 40 °C</td>
  </tr>
</table>

<p><u>Device:</u></p>

<ul>
  <li>Initial zero-setting device (± 10.1 kg)</li>
  <li>Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4% of Max)</li>
  <li>Zero-tracking (≤ 4% of Max)</li>
  <li>Preset tare device (≤ 5 kg)</li>
</ul>

<p><u>Interfaces:</u></p>

<ul>
  <li>RS232C</li>
  <li>USB Host</li>
  <li>USB Slave</li>
  <li>Ethernet</li>
  <li>Wireless communication (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth)</li>
</ul>

<p><u>Software:</u></p>

<p>The legally relevant software is designated version IB770S-1.x, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.</p>

<p><u>Sealing:</u></p>

<p>The enclosure of the weighing part is sealed with void seals.</p>
