Characteristics: This indicating device is designated the 1080. It is designed to be used as part of a Class III or IIII non-automatic weighing instrument. The indicator is self-indicating, dc-powered and operates as a single interval instrument.

Main features:

- Processor and converter unit comprising CPU and A/D converter
- LED display with 7 digits
- LED indicators
- Operator keypad
- Metallic enclosure

Devices:

- Semi-automatic zero setting (≤ 20% Max)
- Zero tracking (≤ 4% Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing
- Automatic tare weighing
- Determination of stability of equilibrium
- Accumulation
- Counting
- Target weighing
- Checkweighing
- Axle weighing
- Gross, Net, Tare, Print, Zero, Motion, Accumulation, Over/Under weight and Network indicators
