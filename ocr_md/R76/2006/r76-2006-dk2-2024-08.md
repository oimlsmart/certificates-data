## Descriptive annex

**Characteristics**

- Accuracy class
  I or II
- Single interval, multiinterval(dual)
- Maximum number of verification scale intervals:
  ≤ 250 000
- Maximum capacity
  from 42 g to 6200 g
- Scale interval
  e = 0.001 g or e = 0.01 g or
  e = 0.1 g
- Display scale interval:
  d = 0.1e or
  d= 0.01e for e=0,001g
- Minimum capacity
  100d for class I
  50d for e = 0.1g and
  20d for e = 0.01 g for class II
- Maximum tare effect:
  ≤-Max
- Temperature range.
  15 °C to +30 °C
- Power supply:
  100-240 VAC, 50/60 Hz using
  external adapter with 24 V
  output.
