**Software**

The software version is displayed during the start-up of the indicator.  
The version format is rx.yy.zz, where x is the legal version no., while yy and zz are major and minor version numbers for changes and corrections not influencing the legal function of the software.  
The tested software version is r1.00.01

**Event counter**

The TLM8 weight indicator/transmitter has a non-resettable Event counter, which increments each time the configuration is changed, or a calibration is performed.

**Tilt compensation**

The indicator can compensate for a tilt of the load receptor, if this is equipped with a tilt sensor, which communicate with the indicator using the serial interface.

The following tilt sensors may be used:

- DPG-Series inclinometer from MEAS Deutschland GmbH / TE Connectivity
- DPL/DPN-Series inclinometer from MEAS Deutschland GmbH / TE Connectivity
- NS-15/PL2-S Dual Axis inclinometer from HL-Planartechnik GmbH / TE Connectivity
- ACx-xxx-2-SCxx-xxx-xx inclinometer from Posital
- ACx-xxx-2-SVxx-xxx-xx inclinometer from Posital
- TILTIX Series inclinometer from Posital
- SOLAR-2 Series Dual Axis inclinometer from Level Developments Ltd.
- BWM826 Series Dual Axis inclinometer from Bewis Sensing Technology LLC.
- BWM827 Series Dual Axis inclinometer from Bewis Sensing Technology LLC
