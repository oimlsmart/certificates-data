**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The FZ series is class I, self-indicating and non-automatic weighing instrument.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The mass detection method of the FZ series is an electromagnetic force balance sensor.

<u>Device:</u>

- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interface:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One or serval of the following interfaces may be incorporated:

- Serial data interface RS232C (to connect Printer)
- USB interface (to connect Personal computer)

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version P-4.xxx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Access to the legally relevant software and parameters is prevented by wire type seals.
