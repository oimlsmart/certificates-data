**Software**

Pressing “TARE” during self-checking will show the legal software version in the display.

Legal software version is: 2.00.

Non-legal application software version is: v1.xxx(y) where x can be 0-9 and y can be a to z.

The scales have software separation.

**Devices**

- Zero-setting range: ±2 % of Max.
- Automatic zero-tracking range: ±2 % of Max.
- Initial zero-setting range: ±10 % of Max.
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare
- Price Look Up (XP… only)
- Unit weight look up (XD… only)
- Check Weighing device (XD… only)
- Counting device (XD… only)
- Percentage weighing device (XD… only)
- Totalization device
- Gravity compensation
- Printing device (can be connected)

**Interfaces**

- RS 232
- Ethernet (Optional)
- RJ11 (optional)
- USB (optional)
- Wi-Fi (optional)
- Bluetooth (optional)
