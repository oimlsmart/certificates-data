<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

The ABS-960+ Baggage Scale is a Class III, self-indicating, single interval, non-automatic weighing instrument.

The instrument may be used for direct sales to the public.

<u>Construction</u>:

The ABS-960+ Baggage Scale comprises an Operator Panel (OP-960+) connected to a load receptor. A Passenger Panel (PP-950-960) may also be connected via a RS-485 connection.

The Operator Panel has the following features:

- ABS plastic enclosure
- Monochrome LCD display
- 10 operator functions keys
- Connections and ports located at the back

The Passenger Panel has the following features:

- Stainless steel front case
- ABS plastic enclosure
- LED display
- Connection port to the Operator Panel located at the back

The load receptor may be fitted with a motorised conveyor.

<u>Devices</u>:

- Initial zero-setting (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium
- Display checking at power-up
- Totalisation function
- Bag counting
- Acting upon significant faults
- Remote operation via computer

<u>Load cell</u>:

The load receptor is fitted with four Anyload load cells model 563YH, E<sub>max</sub> = 500kg.

<u>Rated operating conditions</u>:

The indicator operates directly on a 230VAC supply or via a remote power supply adaptor (7.5 VDC).

The temperature range for the instrument is -10 °C / +40 °C.

<u>Software</u>:

The software is designated version 1.01.xx (where xx refers to the identification of non-legally relevant software, which may be modified by the manufacturer), and is displayed at power-up.

<u>Interfaces</u>

The instrument may have the following interface types:

- RS-485 (passenger panel)
- RS-232 (printer, computer)
- Control inputs/outputs
- USB

<u>Sealing</u>:

Access to the electronics and calibration switches is prevented by sealing the Operator Panel enclosure using a tamper-evident method. The load cell connection is sealed using a tamper-evident method.
