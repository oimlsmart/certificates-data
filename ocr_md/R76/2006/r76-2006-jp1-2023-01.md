**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument</u>:

&nbsp;&nbsp;The GX-L series is a class *II*, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument.
&nbsp;&nbsp;The mass detection method of the GX-L series is an electromagnetic force balance sensor.

<u>Device</u>:

- Initial zero-setting device (≤ 20 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Zero-tracking (≤ 4 % of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing (T = - Max)
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interface</u>:

&nbsp;&nbsp;One or serval of the following interfaces may be incorporated:

- USB interface (to connect Personal computer)
- Serial data interface RS232C (to connect Printer)
- Bluetooth interface (to connect Personal computer)
