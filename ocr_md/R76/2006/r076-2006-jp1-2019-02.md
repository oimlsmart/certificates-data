**DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX**

<u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The AD-6106R and AD-6107R series is a class III, self-indicating, non-automatic weighing instrument. The instruments are not designed for direct sales to the public.

<u>Technical data:</u>

<table>
  <tr><td>Type</td><td>AD-6106R</td><td>AD-6107R</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Class</td><td colspan="2" align="center">III</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Max</td><td colspan="2" align="center">150/200 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>e</td><td colspan="2" align="center">0.1/0.2 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>n</td><td colspan="2" align="center">1500/1000</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Min</td><td colspan="2" align="center">2 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Maximum tare-balancing</td><td colspan="2" align="center">90 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Preset tare</td><td colspan="2" align="center">90 kg</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Temperature range</td><td colspan="2" align="center">+ 5 to + 35 ℃</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Power supply</td><td>AC adapter and/or<br>Dry battery and/or<br>Rechargeable battery</td><td>AC adapter and/or<br>Dry battery</td></tr>
</table>

<u>Device:</u>

- Initial zero-setting device (≤10% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero-setting device (≤4% of Max)
- Zero-tracking device (≤4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing device
- Preset tare device
- Zero indicator
- Indication of stable equilibrium device

<u>Interfaces:</u>

- Serial data interface RS232C (to connect Printer)
- Bluetooth (to connect Personal computer)

<u>Software:</u>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The legally relevant software is designated version P-1.xx, with x reflecting non-legally relevant changes.

<u>Sealing:</u>

Access to the load cell, electronics and calibration switch is prevented by sealing the enclosure using a tamper-evident method.
