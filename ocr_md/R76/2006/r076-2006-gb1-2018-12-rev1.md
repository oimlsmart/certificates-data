<p><strong>DESCRIPTIVE ANNEX</strong></p>

<p><u>Characteristics of the instrument:</u></p>

<p>These indicating devices are designated the DD1050, DD1050i, DD2050 and DD2060X-Series. They are designed to be used as part of a Class III or IIII non-automatic weighing instrument. They operate as single interval, multi-interval or multi-range, are self-indicating and mains or DC-powered.</p>

<p><u>Main features:</u></p>

<p>DD1050</p>

<ul>
<li>Stainless steel and ABS enclosure</li>
<li>10.4” graphic LCD display</li>
<li>A/D conversion module (up to 4)</li>
<li>Main board with processor</li>
</ul>

<p>DD1050i</p>

<ul>
<li>Stainless steel enclosure</li>
<li>10.4” graphic LCD display</li>
<li>A/D conversion module (up to 4)</li>
<li>Main board with processor</li>
<li>Enclosed external power supply</li>
</ul>

<p>DD2050</p>

<ul>
<li>Stainless steel enclosure</li>
<li>10.4” graphic LCD display</li>
<li>A/D conversion module (up to 4)</li>
<li>Main board with processor</li>
</ul>

<p>DD2060X-Series</p>

<ul>
<li>Stainless steel enclosure</li>
<li>10.4” graphic LCD display</li>
<li>A/D conversion module (up to 4)</li>
<li>Main board with processor (CPU based on x86 Architecture and Windows 10 IOT)</li>
</ul>

<p><u>Devices:</u></p>

<ul>
<li>Initial zero setting</li>
<li>Semi-automatic zero setting</li>
<li>Zero tracking</li>
<li>Automatic zero setting (negative load indications for more than 5 s)</li>
<li>Semi-automatic subtractive tare weighing</li>
<li>Determination of stability of equilibrium</li>
<li>Indication of stability of equilibrium</li>
<li>Zero indicator</li>
<li>Preset tare</li>
<li>MPP Alibi storage device</li>
<li>Up to 4 load receptors</li>
<li>Load receptors summation</li>
<li>“Weighbridge modes”:
<ul>
<li>AdR (or RDA): stores vehicle information except weights IN and OUT</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li>RcD: unknown vehicles, stores weight IN until weight OUT captured, record then deleted</li>
<li>RpD (or RCP): stores vehicle information including weight IN</li>

<li>Identification and recognition of the digital load cells and junction box type DILINK: the instrument records the unique S/N of each digital load cell and/or the A/D Converter unique identification number of DILINK junction box for each weighing channel, a new calibration is required if these numbers are not recognised at power up</li>
<li>Weight data transmission in open networks or wireless transmission in protected mode utilising protocols (encrypted transmission of weight data to a remote indicator). The load receptor and the indicator displaying the weighing result must be simultaneously visible to the operator (directly or indirectly) in this configuration.</li>
<li>Optional connection to an external PC and subsequent printing of measurement data from the PC using the data from the MPP Alibi storage device on the indicator</li>
<li>DD2050: optional connection to an external PC allowing printing of measurement data stored on an external PC using the data from the MPP Alibi storage device on the indicator, using the integrated printer on the DD2050</li>
<li>Connection to digital load cells type CPD-M</li>
<li>Connection to smart junction box type DILINK</li>
</ul>

<p><u>Load cell:</u></p>

<p>Any compatible load cell(s) may be used providing the following conditions are met:</p>

<ul>
<li>There is a respective OIML Certificate of Conformity (R60) issued for the load cell.</li>
<li>The certificate contains the load cell types and the necessary load cell data required for the manufacturer’s declaration of compatibility of modules, and any particular installation requirements. A load cell marked NH is allowed only if humidity testing to R76 has been conducted on this load cell.</li>
<li>The compatibility of the load cells and indicator is established by the manufacturer by means of the compatibility of modules calculation at the time of verification.</li>
<li>The load cell transmission conforms to a standard type.</li>
</ul>

<p>Digital load cells type CPD-M and junction box type DILINK may be connected to the indicator.</p>
