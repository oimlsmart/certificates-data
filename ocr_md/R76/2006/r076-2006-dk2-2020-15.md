## Descriptive annex

**Characteristics**

Type:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;IP20a / IP20b / PEa / PEb  
Accuracy class:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;III  
Weighing range:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Single interval, multi interval (dual) or multirange (dual)  
Maximum number of Verification  
Scale Intervals:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≤ 3000&nbsp; or 2x3000  
Maximum capacity:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;6 kg to 30 kg  
Minimum capacity:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;20 × eᵢ  
Maximum tare effect:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-Max within display limits  
Verification scale interval (e=):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;≥ 1 g  
Power supply:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;100-240 VAC.  
Operational temperature:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;-10 °C to +40 °C  

[Watermark: OIML Certification System — large OIML CS logo]

**Software**

The software is separated in weighing software, application software, printing software and keyboard software.

The software version of the different softwares are shown under system settings in the menu.

The approved software versions are,  
weighing software \*):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;V2.10  
application software \*):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A1.xx.yy(z)  
screen keyboard:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;K1.xx  
printing software:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;P4.01.xx  
\*) part of legal relevant software.  
Where xx and yy can be 00 to 99, while z can be not present or a to z.

**Devices**

- Initial zero setting device (≤ 20% of Max)
- Semi-automatic zero setting device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Zero tracking device (≤ 4% of Max)
- Semi-automatic subtractive tare balancing device
- Preset tare
- Gravity compensation device
- Stable equilibrium, Zero, Net and active range indicators.

**Interfaces**

- RS232
- USB
- Ethernet
- Cash drawer
- WiFi (optional)
