## Characteristics

Type: ProCuity MedSurg Bed

Accuracy Class: I IIII

Weighing ranges: Single-interval

Maximum capacity (Max): 250 kg

Minimum capacity (Min): 10 e

Verification scale interval (e=): 2 kg

Maximum tare effect: - 60 kg

Operating temperature range: 5 $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $ to 35 $ ^{\circ} \mathrm{C} $

Power supply: 100-240VAC 50/60Hz

- Semi-automatic subtractive tare device

- Exchange equipment function $ ^{*} $

- Weight logging device

- Operator information messages

- Gravity compensation device (sealed)

- Stable equilibrium and Net indicators.

*) Lock the present net weight and take any change in the gross weight as a change in tare weight.
