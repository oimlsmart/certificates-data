**Measuring system description:**

The CFS Fuel Star Series fuel dispensers consist of a FSU pumping unit with gas elimination device, FMU measuring transducer, CPS electronic calculator, electromagnetic valve, sight glass and house with delivery nozzle. There is version for installation in centrally pumped system. Satellite delivery point can be present.

There are two types of subsystems:

- Standard Duty with Q<sub>max</sub> 50 L/min, witch contains one FSU-50 pumping unit and one FMU-50 measuring transducer
- Heavy Duty with Q<sub>max</sub> 90 L/min, witch contains one FSU-90 pumping unit and one FMU-90 measuring transducer.
