Up to four hydraulic circuits are housed in a single enclosure with

1. one common Tulip calculator
2. measuring transducers of type Tulip pulser, or Eltomatic ME01-04
3. optional features and functions:

   communications (remote automation)

   electronic or electromechanical totalizers,
   speakers, preset, keypad, pushbutton options
   slave displays, and/or master-satellite operation
   additional displays, number of display digits and display size (minimum 6 digits money and volume, 4 digits unit price, and minimum height 25mm)
