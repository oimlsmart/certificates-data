Each measuring instrument consists at least of:

- One combined pump and gas eliminator device (gas separator);
- One measurement transducer (meter);
- One calculating/indicating device (calculator).

The characteristics of the mentioned parts of the fuel dispenser are presented at table 3 and higher.

The same housing of the dispenser can comprise of one or more measuring systems. When more than one measuring systems are in one housing, one calculating/indicating device may be a common part of the measuring systems.

For multi-product dispensers it is only possible to deliver one product at the same time on one side of the dispenser.

Table 2 gives an overview of flow characteristics of the configurations of the family of instruments.
