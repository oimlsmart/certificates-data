**Characteristics of the density sensor:**

The density sensor is based on the Coriolis principle for the determination of the density of the liquid. The only approved output of the density sensor is the tube period.  
Together with the calibration constants, a flow computer, to which the density sensor is connected and to which optionally external process pressure and temperature transmitters are connected, can calculate the liquid density under metering conditions and/or under standard conditions.
