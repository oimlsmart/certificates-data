**R117-1: 2007 ; R117-2: 2014 ; R117-3: 2014**

This OIML Certificate relates only to metrological and technical characteristics of the type of measuring instrument covered by the relevant OIML Recommendation identified above.

This OIML Certificate does not bestow any form of legal international approval.
