In Tables 1 to 6, the general characteristics of the measuring instrument are presented.
The construction of the measurement transducer is recorded in documentation folders TC7149-7 for the measurement sensor and TC10822-3 for the electronics.

**Table 1 General characteristics applicable to all Promass measurement sensors**

- Density range: 400 … 1400 kg/m³
- Maximum viscosity: 1000 mPa·s
- Accuracy class: 0.3; 0.5; 1.0 and 1.5
- Environmental classes: M3 / E2 / H3
- Ambient temperature range: -40 … +55 °C
- Intended for the measurement of: Oil and oil products, chemicals, potable liquids, liquefied gases under pressure measured at temperatures above -10 °C, liquified gases under pressure measured below -10 °C, liquefied carbon dioxide and liquefied natural gas (LNG).
