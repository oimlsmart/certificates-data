**Characteristics of the measuring instrument**  
In Table 1 the general characteristics of the calculating and indicating device are presented.

The calculating and indicating device can be configured for measuring up to two streams and controlling single/dual loading arm configuration with a single device. The pulse cable and the analog I/O cable should be less than 30 meters in length.

The construction of the calculating and indicating device is recorded in the Documentation folder number NMi-2324978-01-1.
