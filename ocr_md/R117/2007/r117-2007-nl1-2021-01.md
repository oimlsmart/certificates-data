**Table 1 General characteristics applicable to all Promass measurement sensors**

- Density range:                    400 … 1400 kg/m³  
- Maximum viscosity:                1000 mPa·s

- Environmental classes:            M3 / E2 / H3  
- Ambient temperature range:        -40 … +55 °C
