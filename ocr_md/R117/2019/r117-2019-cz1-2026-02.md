# Page 3 Summary — OIML Certificate R117/2019-A-CZ1-26.02

This page contains the **Measuring device description** and **Main components** sections:

## Key Content

**Device Types:**
- Fuel dispensers and AdBlue dispensers of type series **Smartline, Cosmic, and MEPBLUE**, produced under trademarks **MEPSAN** or **MITES**
- Versions differ only in housing appearance; same internal components
- Can be fuel dispensers, AdBlue dispensers, or a combination

**Applications:**
- Fuel dispensers: refueling motor vehicles, small boats, and small aircraft
- AdBlue dispensers: refilling SCR tanks for NOₓ reduction in diesel engines (flow limited to 50 L/min)

**Three Types of Fuel Measuring Systems:**
1. **Standard** — Qmax 50 L/min (one pumping unit, one transducer; can supply two simultaneous measuring systems)
2. **High speed** — Qmax 90 L/min (larger pumping unit)
3. **Ultra high-speed** — Qmax 130 L/min (two pumping units + two transducers in parallel, dispensed via one nozzle)

**Installation Requirements (when no gas separator is installed):**
- Level detection system for minimum tank level
- Delivery delayed until submerged pump runs ≥3 seconds
- Pipelines with positive slope ≥1%
- Non-return valve upstream of each measurement transducer

**Main Components Table:**

| Component | Model |
|-----------|-------|
| Pumping unit with gas separator | MLB PUMP |
| Measurement transducer | MLB Meter (MLB Meter anodized for AdBlue) |
| Pulse transmitter & calculating unit | LUMINA – Pulser Unit |
| Indicating device | LUMINA – Display Unit |
| ATC unit (optional) | MEP-SENS T |
