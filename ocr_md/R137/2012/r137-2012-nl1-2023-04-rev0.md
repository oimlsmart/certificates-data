**Installation conditions:**
*Installation requirements*
Any components which could affect the gas flow must be avoided within the prescribed inlet pipe length. The inlet pipe must be designed as a straight pipe section of a nominal diameter <3% difference compared to the gas meter.

The installation requirements, based on accuracy class and mild and/or severe flow disturbance are specified in table 2. The used flow conditioner is of type: CPA55E.

*Working pressure*
The spool piece and the transducers can be used up to the specified pressure as given in table 1.

The following working pressure ranges are applicable:
- For a working pressure range (p<sub>min</sub>) ≥10 bar(g) a calibration on one pressure is sufficient. The working pressure range (p<sub>min</sub> and p<sub>max</sub>) as given on the name plate is allowed to be within ½·p<sub>fix</sub> and 2·p<sub>fix</sub>.
- For a working pressure range (p<sub>min</sub>) <10 bar(g) a calibration shall be performed on the two pressures at p<sub>min</sub> and p<sub>max</sub> as given on the name plate.

The ultrasonic gas meter does not make use of an internal pressure or temperature sensor. The correct fixed density (ρ<sub>fix</sub>) and fixed pressure setting (p<sub>fix</sub>) shall be applied before calibration in the USM setting.

For a maximum working pressure (p<sub>max</sub>) of the ultrasonic gas meter above 60 bar(a), a maximum calibration pressure (p<sub>max calibration</sub>) of around 60 bar(a) is sufficient since this is the highest possible test pressure at any test location traceable to (inter)national standards.

*Thermowell*
A thermowell may be mounted at ≥ 1,5D and ≤ 5D from the outlet of the meter in unidirectional flow measurement.

*Alternative welded configuration of the gas meter*
The central meter body can be welded directly onto the flanges or to inlet and outlet pipes. The welding may not cause more than a 3% diameter step. The meter shall be installed as stated in "*Installation requirements*". The central meter body, including welded piping or welded flanges, shall be calibrated as a meter package during the examination for putting into use of the gas meter.
