Remark [1] and [2]: the flow rates in m³/h, as stated on the type plate, are calculated based on the actual inner diameter of the ultrasonic gas meter. The corresponding flow rates in m³/h can be calculated as follows:

Q = v · ¼ · π · D<sup>2</sup> · 3600

Where:  
Q = flow rate [m³/h]  
v = velocity [m/s]  
D = internal diameter [m]

Higher values of Q<sub>min</sub> and lower values of Q<sub>max</sub> are allowed on condition that Q<sub>min</sub> ≤ 0,05 Q<sub>max</sub> and Q<sub>max</sub> / Q<sub>t</sub> ≥ 5.

**Installation conditions:**  
The meter needs to be installed according to the following configuration for mild and severe flow disturbance for accuracy class 0,5:

Mild and Severe flow disturbance:

- Upstream: 10D + Zanker + 5D of straight pipe.
- Downstream a minimum of 5D straight pipes.
- The flow conditioner shall be a Zanker compliant design.

The detailed installation requirements and construction can be located in the EU-Type examination certificate T12483.

**The calibration of the meters shall be performed as follows:**

- When the working pressure range (p<sub>min</sub> … p<sub>max</sub>) is within 52 bar, one calibration on one pressure (p<sub>test</sub>) suffices.
- When the working pressure range is >52 bar, two calibrations shall be performed at the minimum (p<sub>min</sub>) and maximum (p<sub>max</sub>) operating pressure. Where p<sub>max, test</sub> is limited to around 60 bar since all traceable high pressure natural gas test locations are world wide limited to this maximum test pressure.
