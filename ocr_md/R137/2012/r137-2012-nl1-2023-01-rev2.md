**Installation conditions:**

*Installation requirements*
The meter needs to be installed according the following configurations:
- 6-path meters:
  - Upstream:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a minimum of 5D + FC + 5D of straight pipe.<br>The flow conditioner (FC) shall be a CPA type 50E compliant design.
  - Downstream:&nbsp;&nbsp;a minimum of 5D
- 8-path meters:
  - Upstream:&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a minimum of 10D of straight pipe without a flow conditioner.
  - Downstream:&nbsp;&nbsp;a minimum of 5D

*Bi-directional flow measurement*
- 6-path meters

During conformity assessment it is sufficient to verify a bi-directional meter in one direction only. For bi-directional flow measurement the outlet pipe and flow conditioner shall be identical to the inlet. The installation of a temperature sensor is at 2–5D from the outlet of the meter. For bi-directional applications, an additional temperature sensor can be installed 2–5D upstream of the meter. For bi-directional applications, the meter and pipe spools including the thermowell(s), shall be calibrated as a meter package during the examination for putting into use of the gas meter.
- 8-path meters

During conformity assessment, the meter shall be calibrated in both directions if the meter is intended to be used for bi-directional measurements. The requirements shall be met without modification of the meter’s parameter settings.
