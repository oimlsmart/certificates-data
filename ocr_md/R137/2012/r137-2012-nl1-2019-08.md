**Installation conditions:**
Inlet piping and flow conditioner:
The gas meter is used with the indicated minimum piping lengths:

- Inlet conditions
  - 10D without a flow conditioner;
  - 2D – Krohne flow conditioner – 3D
- Outlet condition
  - 3D, with temperature sensor at least 2D downstream
- Bi-directional use
  - For bi-directional use, the temperature sensor shall be at least 4D upstream of the meter.
