**Installation conditions:**
The meter can operate in the following positions: horizontal flow, vertical flow up and vertical flow down.

Any components which cause severe flow disturbances and could affect the gas flow must be avoided within the extra prescribed inlet pipe length which is 2 DN. The inlet pipe must be designed as a straight pipe section of the same nominal diameter as the gas meter.

For mild flow disturbances there is no prescribed extra inlet pipe length necessary.
